# Rejected: Robin to Kamabakka for the Timeskip

## Idea

Send Robin to Kamabakka because identity and self-expression could connect thematically to her history of aliases, hiding, and survival.

## Why Peak Piece rejected the destination

The thematic connection is not enough to explain why Kuma would send Robin there when he:

- knows the Revolutionary Army
- knows where its strategic infrastructure is
- knows Robin is historically and politically valuable to them

Peak Piece does not move a character to a location merely because the theme can be made to rhyme.

## What survives

Identity material is not rejected.

Ivankov belongs to the Revolutionary Army.

If Robin and Ivankov have something useful to explore, they can meet organically through the organization.

The destination does not need to carry the theme by itself.
