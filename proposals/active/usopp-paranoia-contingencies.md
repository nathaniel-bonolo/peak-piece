# Proposal: Usopp's Contingency Paranoia

## Seed

The Evil Zoro encounter may teach Usopp a dangerous second lesson:

> **Even the strongest person protecting me can become the threat.**

That could turn healthy preparation into obsessive contingency planning.

## Possible behavior

Usopp quietly starts thinking through how he would:

- escape Luffy
- immobilize Sanji
- disable Franky's weapons
- slow Brook
- avoid Robin
- survive Zoro

The important point is not that his plans are perfect.

The important point is that he feels compelled to have them.

## Why this helps

Usopp's greatest strength would gain a cost.

Preparation can become paranoia.

His arc would therefore avoid:

> coward -> Batman -> flawless tactical god

and instead become:

> fear -> preparation -> competence -> obsession -> learning that preparation cannot make life safe

## Not locked

The current conversation only locks this as a strong possibility.

Do not write it into canon character behavior until the Evil Zoro material is audited directly.
