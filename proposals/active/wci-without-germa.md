# Proposal: Whole Cake Island Without Germa

Status: Active

## Premise

Remove the requirement for Sanji's Germa backstory and rebuild Whole Cake Island from the remaining narrative functions.

## Functions canon Germa performed

Current review identified several functions that need to be reconsidered rather than blindly preserved:

- motive behind Sanji's wedding
- Sanji backstory material
- found-family versus blood-family theme
- stonefish sequence / forced early docking
- timing of Pudding's reveal

## Current Peak direction

Do not assume Sanji still needs to be a groom.

Whole Cake Island can still contain a major Charlotte wedding as political infrastructure without making Sanji the person getting married.

Sanji's capture can instead emerge from Charlotte intelligence and soul harvesting.

## Starvation / food logistics

Without Sanji, the Straw Hats should suffer real logistical consequences.

This may force an early stop at Cacao Island without making Nami incompetent.

## Pudding

Pudding should not exist only because she is a girl who wants Sanji.

Her core function in Peak WCI is currently:

- memory intelligence
- fear extraction
- interrogation support
- family loyalty
- identity cost

Her relationship with Sanji can still matter, but it should not be the only reason she is in the arc.

## Open questions

- Does WCI still feature a political wedding?
- Who is marrying whom?
- What specific event causes Sanji's capture?
- What exact information does Pudding gain from him?
