# Proposal: Chopper, SMILE and Blackbeard

## Accepted direction around Chopper

[[characters/chopper/overview|Chopper]] researches SMILE throughout Wano and concludes that symptom treatment is not enough.

The artificial Devil Fruit effect itself must be removed or neutralized.

## Blackbeard problem

Blackbeard has demonstrated a way to remove or steal Devil Fruits.

The known process kills the victim.

That makes him medically relevant.

The alliance may need to **capture Blackbeard alive**, discover how his extraction works, and find a way to separate the useful principle from the lethal process.

## Character objectives

### Chopper

Needs Blackbeard alive long enough to understand the technique.

### Kid

Has a direct reason to cooperate because Killer needs a cure.

This forces Kid into another Emperor-targeting alliance after his previous experiences.

### Law

Blackbeard destroyed Law's crew.

Law may have to restrain the desire to kill him because his Fruit could be necessary for nonlethal extraction.

### Luffy

Has his own history and strategic reasons to fight Blackbeard.

## Important design rule

Blackbeard should not simply hand Chopper the cure.

A stronger structure is:

- Blackbeard provides the missing extraction principle
- Law provides extraordinary surgical capability
- Chopper understands the pathology and creates the treatment

## Open questions

- what Blackbeard's extraction actually does
- why it currently kills
- how Law can assist without solving the entire problem
- what damage remains after the artificial Fruit is removed
- whether victims need separate rehabilitation afterward
