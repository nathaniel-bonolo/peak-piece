# Proposal: May Weller

Status: Active

This is a collaborator OC and is not Peak canon yet.

## Current concept

May Weller is an East Blue character descended from a disowned Charlotte.

Major affiliations in the current pitch:

- Baroque Works
- Whitebeard family
- Charlotte family connection

She has a long-standing crush on Miss All Sunday / Nico Robin.

She later undergoes memory alteration involving Pudding.

## Zhenniao Fruit

The collaborator considers the bird concept load-bearing.

Current compromise direction:

> **May is a Zhenniao Zoan whose body turns substances she consumes into toxins or medicines.**

The bird concept should remain coherent rather than becoming an excuse for unrelated Mythical Zoan powers.

Proposed biological grounding includes:

- waterfowl / merganser-like ancestry
- fast terrestrial hunting adaptation
- snake-heavy diet
- oily toxin delivery
- up to five stored toxin types
- impact vulnerability
- fire vulnerability

## Dissociation

The collaborator's original concept gives the Zoan a strong independent consciousness and memory separation.

Peak review currently prefers a narrower version:

- one autobiographical memory system
- trauma-created compartmentalization
- Zoan instinct may influence dissociative states
- May names a survival state "the bird"
- different states may access memories through different associative routes

This is not resolved.

## Pudding connection

This character could become an important formative failure for Pudding.

Instead of a simple "separate magical hard drive," Pudding may discover that memories are networked.

Cutting one association does not necessarily remove every route into the same experience.

This could help establish why memory editing risks changing identity.

## Charlotte Cordial

The current pitch includes a state/personality called Charlotte Cordial produced after memory alteration.

Potentially strong direction:

Cordial should not simply be "evil assassin May."

She may genuinely experience the Charlotte family as home while another state experiences the same history as captivity.

Neither state has to be fake.

## Risk

Avoid making May secretly central to every major faction.

The current "Baroque Works -> Whitebeards -> Charlotte ancestry" chain can work if she remains low in institutional importance.

Do not keep adding major hidden connections.
