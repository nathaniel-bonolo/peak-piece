# Gems: Design Audit

There is no direct canon equivalent. Gems solve the rewrite's need for a finite strategic energy resource that makes exceptional output, industry, Haki, Devil Fruit scale, and geopolitics interact.

## Failure mode
The obvious danger is mana accounting and Policy Analysis Piece. Gem budgets belong mostly backstage. Show exhaustion, shortages, choices, and consequences instead of spreadsheets.

> **The spreadsheet is ours. The audience gets the adventure.**
