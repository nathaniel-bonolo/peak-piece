# Luffy: Relationships

## Shanks

Shanks remains one of the most important people in Luffy's life.

The relationship gains tension as Luffy's understanding of piracy grows.

Love and questioning can coexist.

The eventual hat return should include Luffy finally asking the questions he has been carrying.

## Gol D. Roger

Roger begins as a mythic predecessor.

Over time Luffy becomes interested in the actual man and in what Roger believed he was doing when he started the Great Pirate Era.

## Boa Hancock

Boa helps Luffy because she is utterly infatuated with him, but Peak Piece lets that affection produce competent action.

After Marineford she refuses to let him return to the New World unprepared.

She organizes his Haki education and personally teaches Conqueror's.

A future romance or marriage is **not locked**.

## Straw Hats

The crew helps turn Luffy's simple instincts into functioning behavior.

His fleet philosophy should emerge from how the crew acts, not from Luffy writing a constitution.
