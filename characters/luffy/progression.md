# Luffy: Progression

## Early sea

Luffy knows pirates can be monsters.

He fights people like Arlong and Crocodile without concluding that piracy itself is the entire problem.

His answer is still:

> Those are bad pirates. I will be a good one.

## Skypiea: snapping point

Skypiea changes the category of the question.

Enel shows Luffy that domination does not require piracy.

Power itself can become the problem when nobody can resist the person holding it.

This does not instantly produce a speech or ideology.

It changes what Luffy begins to notice.

## Building a meaning for his flag

Luffy gradually stops treating a pirate flag as only a symbol of personal freedom.

If somebody sails under his flag, that should mean something.

That becomes the basis of [[lore/factions/straw-hat-fleet|the Straw Hat Fleet]].

## Roger and the Pirate Era

As Luffy sees more of the world created by the Great Pirate Era, a harder question grows:

> **What did Roger think would happen?**

Roger's final act did not only inspire adventurers.

It also unleashed predators.

Luffy increasingly wants to find the One Piece partly because he wants to understand what Roger was thinking.

## Shanks and the hat

Shanks gave Luffy his childhood definition of a great pirate.

Luffy still loves him.

But the hat gains questions.

Returning it is no longer an automatic graduation ceremony.

When they meet again, Luffy wants to ask Shanks what he believed piracy meant and what responsibility comes with the freedom he taught Luffy to worship.

## Thesis

Luffy begins believing freedom is enough.

He grows toward understanding:

> **Freedom tells you what nobody else is allowed to do to you. It does not automatically tell you what you are allowed to do to everybody else.**

He remains Luffy while learning the second half.
