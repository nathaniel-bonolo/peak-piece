# Luffy: Abilities

## Gum-Gum Fruit

Luffy is rubber.

The Fruit remains a Paramecia.

Peak Piece removes:

- the hidden Nika Fruit
- the Mythical Zoan retcon
- Joy Boy owning the same secret Fruit
- reality-warping cartoon physics as Luffy's awakening

## Awakening

Current locked direction:

> **Luffy can store part of the elastic deformation energy created when his rubber body is stretched, compressed, or struck, then release that stored energy through another part of his body.**

This is not generic energy absorption.

The interaction must come from the rubber body actually deforming.

## Damage still matters

Luffy still:

- feels pain
- takes physical damage
- can be concussed
- can exceed what his body can safely store
- can be injured faster than he can exploit the stored energy

A stronger opponent hitting him is not simply charging his battery for free.

## Gear 5

There is no current Peak Piece Gear 5.

The name is not banned forever.

If a fifth stage naturally grows out of established rubber mechanics and Luffy's progression, it can earn the name later.

The story will not invent a fifth stage merely because Gear 4 exists.

## Haki

Luffy's post-Marineford Haki training happens through [[lore/locations/amazon-lily|Amazon Lily]], not two years under Rayleigh.

See [[story/interludes/timeskip-haki-training|Timeskip Haki Training]].
