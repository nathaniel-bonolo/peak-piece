# Roronoa Zoro: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Zoro begins as Pirate Hunter Zoro, becomes Luffy's swordsman, trains under Mihawk, and later interacts with Wano sword tradition including Shusui.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses Zoro for loyalty, ambition, sword tradition, direct martial resolve, and comic severity.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

The Pirate Hunter title largely stops functioning materially after he joins. Sword exchanges can also risk feeling like stat-stick progression.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Zoro still cashes bounties on genuinely predatory pirates when appropriate, helping fund the crew/fleet. Shusui's return is voluntary and meaningful.

## Why this solution

His original profession is too useful and characterful to become trivia, and it funds Luffy's network without taxation.

## What we preserve

Sword ambition, loyalty, endurance, bounty-hunter history, Wano sword culture, straightforwardness.

## What we intentionally reject

Government bounty as moral truth and weapon swaps treated only as upgrades.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make Zoro a cop. The crew judges behavior, not Marine paperwork.
