# Fisher Tiger: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Fisher Tiger is a former slave who attacks Mary Geoise to free slaves, leads the Sun Pirates, struggles with hatred of humans, and dies after refusing human blood.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon makes him a tragic liberation figure whose heroism coexists with trauma and hatred he does not want children to inherit.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Peak Piece wants the transfusion endpoint to grow more directly out of an earlier liberation mechanism and to leave material consequences in Gem history.

## Peak Piece response

Tiger uses and overuses dangerous Gem injection during an impossible liberation operation. The damage contributes to his later transfusion crisis. He remains capable of hating humans without forcing hatred onto children and occasionally corrects secretly-training Shirahoshi without becoming her formal master.

## Why this solution

One historical action now produces opposite later readings: Hordy sees proof it works; Shirahoshi sees proof of cost.

## What we preserve

Liberation of slaves, Sun Pirates, understandable hatred, refusal to pass hatred cleanly to children, tragic death.

## What we intentionally reject

A transfusion death disconnected from the new Gem system.

## Failure modes to watch

Exact Gem physiology remains open. Do not invent pseudomedical detail just to sound rigorous.
