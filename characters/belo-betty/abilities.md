# Belo Betty: Abilities

## One-sentence rule

> **Betty can amplify resolve that already exists, allowing someone to fully act on courage and capability they are currently suppressing through fear, hesitation, or self-doubt.**

The same logic applies to war because resolve is not restricted to heroic courage.

## Positive and negative directions

If a civilian already wants to resist but believes resistance is impossible, Betty can strengthen commitment to resist.

If a soldier sincerely believes the battle is lost and is already considering retreat or surrender, Betty can strengthen commitment to retreat or surrender.

## Limits

Betty cannot:

- create a belief from nothing
- insert political loyalty
- make somebody care about a cause they reject
- grant skills they do not possess
- make false information true
- permanently override contradictory evidence

The target must already possess a real actionable impulse.

## Conflicting resolve

People can want contradictory things at once. A soldier may want to survive, protect friends, obey an officer, and retreat. Betty is not a clean morale button. Discipline and identity can create competing commitments.

Timing therefore matters enormously.

## Placebo conditioning

Betty can pair genuine activation with a repeatable cue: a speech, gesture, command, or the moment Usopp puts on the Sogeking mask.

After enough repetition she can preserve the cue while withholding the power. Ordinary expectation, conditioning, and muscle memory may reproduce part of the behavioral state.

The Fruit is **not** literally placebo magic. The Fruit establishes the early experience; psychology preserves the learned association later.

## Usopp partnership

[[characters/usopp/overview|Usopp]] manufactures what the enemy believes is happening.

Betty amplifies the resolve to act on what they believe.

That is why their partnership can defeat stronger forces without turning every battle into a bigger punch.
