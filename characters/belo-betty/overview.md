# Belo Betty

## Core

Betty becomes one of the most important people in Usopp's timeskip because her Devil Fruit interacts directly with the gap between knowing what to do and being too afraid to act.

She does not make Usopp competent. Usopp has to build the plan first.

## Usopp

Usopp initially concludes:

> **Betty makes me brave.**

The first operations really do use her Fruit. Later Betty reproduces the same ritual without activating it. Conditioning, expectation, and practiced behavior carry him.

Only after the campaign succeeds does she reveal that he has been operating without the boost for some time.

The revelation is not "I am no longer afraid."

It is:

> **I did all that.**

## Politics

Betty's power fits the Revolutionary Army because it does not manufacture beliefs or loyalty. She amplifies something already present in the person.

People still own the decision.
