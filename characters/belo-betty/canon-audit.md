# Belo Betty: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Betty's Kobu Kobu no Mi lets her encouragement awaken latent strength in others; she rallies civilians in Lulusia.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon makes her literally empowering: a Revolutionary commander whose ability helps oppressed people act instead of replacing them with elite saviors.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

The broad stat-boost framing leaves psychological, morale, and Usopp-specific potential underused.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Betty amplifies actionable resolve that already exists. She cannot create belief, loyalty, skill, or desire. With Usopp she genuinely boosts him early and later preserves the cue without activating the Fruit.

## Why this solution

This keeps agency intact and turns the Fruit into a psychological-warfare tool instead of generic numbers.

## What we preserve

Revolutionary imagery, encouragement, civilian agency, commander role, empowerment.

## What we intentionally reject

Pure stat boosting as the only interesting application.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not turn resolve amplification into mind control. Conflicting motives and new evidence must matter.
