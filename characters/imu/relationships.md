# Imu: Relationships

## Charlotte Linlin

Imu gave or arranged access to the Soul-Soul Fruit and offered protection from Government pressure.

In exchange, Linlin supplies high-quality soul material on a recurring schedule.

The relationship is contractual, strategic, and mutually dangerous.

Linlin is powerful enough to rule an empire and still fears what happens if she fails the contract.
