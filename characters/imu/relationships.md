# Imu: Relationships

## Charlotte Linlin

Imu does not create Linlin's empire.

Linlin is already building the political/family project that becomes Totto Land and already has independent reasons to harvest soul material.

Imu offers:

- World Government tolerance
- protection from the level of suppression that would normally threaten a centuries-long pirate state
- political room for Linlin's project to grow

In exchange, Linlin supplies recurring high-quality soul material that can serve Imu's deferred-consequence system.

This is effectively a tax for immunity.

Whole Cake Island does not simply "feed Imu."

Linlin uses souls for her own:

- longevity
- Homies
- weapons
- state
- family systems

and pays part of that extraction obligation upward because the contract buys freedom from Government destruction.

The relationship is therefore:

> two dangerous powers exchanging something the other uniquely controls

rather than:

> final villain secretly invented the midgame villain.

Current working continuity still allows Imu to have arranged or influenced Linlin's access to the Soul-Soul Fruit, but the empire remains Linlin's creation.
