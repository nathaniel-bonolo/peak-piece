# Queen

## Keep the fun

Queen stays a ridiculous cyborg Brachiosaurus.

Peak Piece is not deleting the insanity that makes him entertaining.

## Actual military job

Queen becomes the Beast Pirates' doctrine / force-design scientist.

Kaido defines the strategic goal.

Caesar produces unstable artificial Zoans.

Queen asks:

> **What can we actually use this for?**

## SMILE classification

Queen stops grading every SMILE only by:

> Did this become a proper Zoan?

He classifies useful outcomes.

Examples:

- low fear response
- abnormal pain tolerance
- aggression
- permanent useful claws or armor
- sensory mutations
- unusually durable bodies
- specialist movement traits

A failed Zoan can still be a useful military phenotype.

Completely debilitating results are failures, not soldiers Kaido feeds Fruit to for no reason.

## Doctrine

Queen can design:

- deployment roles
- support equipment
- stimulants
- restraints
- recovery procedures
- compatibility rules
- training for abnormal bodies
- ways to exploit a phenotype without pretending it is a normal Zoan

This turns biological chaos into an army.

## Chopper

Queen is **not** built as Chopper's designated thematic rival.

Chopper can interact with Queen's work naturally because SMILE matters to him, but Peak Piece will not force a "doctor versus weaponized biology" matchup because the symbolism is convenient.

Chopper's long-term antagonist is the SMILE problem itself.

## Viruses / Ice Oni

The Ice Oni crisis is removed as the disposable problem Chopper solves to justify his presence in Wano.

Virus weapons are not automatically banned forever, but if Queen uses one later it must survive the same strategic scrutiny as every other weapon.

## Germa

Queen's Germa-related narrative function is removed with the Germa rewrite.

## Franky

Peak Piece rejects making Queen Franky's father.

It adds no needed function and makes the world smaller.

> **This is not Nepo Piece.**
