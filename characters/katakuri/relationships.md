# Charlotte Katakuri: Relationships

## Pudding

Katakuri predicts from the outside.

Pudding relives from the inside.

Their abilities produce different forms of understanding and different blind spots.

A compressed payoff for Katakuri's growth may be as small as letting Pudding finish a sentence he already knows she is going to say.
