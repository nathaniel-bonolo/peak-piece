# Jinbe: Progression

Jinbe moves through several models of Fishman survival:

- direct liberation
- legal leverage through the Warlord system
- deterrence under Whitebeard
- stability under Linlin
- alliance with Luffy

Each solves some problems and creates others.

His story should increasingly ask how Fishmen can build security that does not disappear when one protector dies.
