# Jinbe

## Core FMI direction

Jinbe's political life is driven by a practical question:

> **What actually keeps Fishmen alive tomorrow?**

He searches for durable external security rather than relying only on heroic moments.

## Warlord period

Part of Jinbe's reason for becoming a Warlord is to gain legitimate leverage, travel, and security for Fishmen.

He wants Fishmen to move through the surface world without automatically becoming merchandise.

## Whitebeard

[[characters/whitebeard/overview|Whitebeard]] achieves much of what Jinbe had been trying to build.

Whitebeard:

- frees enslaved Fishmen
- returns kidnapped people to families
- creates safe routes
- protects Fishman merchants and travelers
- helps sell Fishman Island's Gems
- splits the profits fairly

That makes Jinbe's willingness to risk his life for Whitebeard at Marineford materially grounded.

Whitebeard did not merely protect an island.

He restored dignity and movement to Jinbe's people.

## After Whitebeard

Whitebeard's death proves how dangerous it is for an entire people to depend on one man's deterrence.

That failure helps shape Jinbe's later decisions.
