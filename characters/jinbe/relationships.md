# Jinbe: Relationships

## Fisher Tiger

Tiger gives Jinbe direct-action liberation and its emotional legacy.

## Whitebeard

Whitebeard turns deterrence into real freedom of movement.

## Neptune

Neptune and Jinbe both understand the pressure behind ugly political compromises.

## Linlin

Linlin restores stability after Whitebeard but converts protection into ownership and extraction.

## Luffy

Luffy eventually offers the possibility of an alliance that changes the balance rather than simply replacing one owner with another.
