# Charlotte Linlin: Relationships

## Pudding

Linlin sees Pudding as both daughter and strategically important memory specialist.

She can be frighteningly demanding while still understanding that Pudding's identity is being damaged by repeated extraction work.

Locked line:

> **I asked you to preserve my soldiers, Pudding. I did not ask you to erase my daughter.**

Potential responses to Pudding grieving enemies:

> Then grieve.

> Understanding my enemies does not make you disloyal to me.

These are not all locked dialogue.

## Katakuri

Katakuri is a centuries-old commander trusted to act autonomously.

Linlin does not micromanage every battlefield decision.

## Imu

The relationship is contractual, strategic, and dangerous.

Linlin fears Imu despite being an emperor in her own right.
