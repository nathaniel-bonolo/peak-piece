# Charlotte Linlin: Progression

Status: Draft

## Current direction

Linlin should not be reduced to "strong villain gets angrier."

Her progression should expose:

- how she defines family
- how she defines ownership
- how she treats sacrifice
- what she believes an empire owes its people
- what happens when her soul economy is turned against her
- what she is willing to spend when extinction becomes possible

Soul Feast should eventually force her to spend part of herself rather than endlessly spending everybody else.
