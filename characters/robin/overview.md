# Nico Robin

## Current direction

Robin's core canon role is already strong:

- archaeologist
- historian
- survivor of state persecution
- reader of Poneglyphs
- investigator of erased history
- someone who understands the difference between official history and lived history

Peak Piece does not need to send her through more torture merely to justify growth.

## Timeskip destination

Robin goes to the [[lore/factions/revolutionary-army|Revolutionary Army]].

Kuma knows the organization and its base.

He sends her there directly.

See [[characters/robin/timeskip|Robin's Timeskip]].

## Why the RA fits

Robin's growth can naturally involve:

- intelligence analysis
- archives
- propaganda
- state secrecy
- underground networks
- political history
- logistics of resistance
- languages
- archaeological evidence
- the World Government's methods of erasure

She does not need to become a generic revolutionary fighter.

Her value is that she understands information other people cannot even read.

## Kamabakka

Robin does not need to be sent to Kamabakka merely because identity is a potentially relevant theme.

Ivankov belongs to the Revolutionary Army anyway.

If identity material becomes useful, Robin can encounter it organically through the organization without a theme-first teleport destination.
