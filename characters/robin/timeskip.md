# Robin: Timeskip

## Arrival

Kuma sends Robin **directly to the Revolutionary Army base**.

Kuma is a top Revolutionary commander. He knows the organization wants Robin and knows where its infrastructure is. Peak Piece removes the Tequila Wolf scenic route because no extra suffering is needed to justify the destination.

## No chosen-one framing

The RA does not treat Robin as a mystical "Light of the Revolution" chosen person.

They value her for concrete reasons:

- Ohara's surviving scholarship
- Poneglyph literacy
- historical reconstruction
- intelligence value
- knowledge of World Government erasure
- experience surviving criminal and state networks

Crucially, usefulness is **not the price of being allowed to remain there**.

## First phase: choice

Robin gets time before becoming a field asset.

She reads, rests, talks to adult peers, explores the archives, and is asked what she wants to work on.

The character question is [[characters/robin/identity|Who am I now that I am allowed to live?]].

## Intelligence development

The RA formalizes skills Robin already developed through survival:

- source reliability
- cross-referencing reports
- propaganda analysis
- archival security
- codes and operational secrecy
- reconstructing events from incomplete evidence
- distinguishing what somebody **knows** from what they merely **believe**
- tracing institutions across changed names and generations

Robin can notice the same machinery repeating across documents that look unrelated to everybody else.

## Hana Hana no Mi as intelligence tool

Her Fruit develops laterally rather than only becoming bigger hands.

Potential applications:

- eyes at multiple observation points
- ears under tables or behind walls
- hands passing small objects through secure spaces
- copying or retrieving documents without entering physically
- manipulating locks, windows, or equipment remotely
- simultaneous observation posts

The natural constraint is cognition. Listening to many conversations and watching many spaces at once should require sensory filtering and compartmentalization.

## Early field mission direction

A strong first mission is **records extraction**, not a boss fight.

A Government-linked office contains records connecting officials to trafficking or another covert system. Destroying the building would erase evidence. Robin becomes essential because she knows which records should survive, which names must be protected, and how apparently boring documents connect.

Exact mission details remain open.

## Usopp reunion

Robin develops independently while [[characters/usopp/timeskip|Usopp]] spends weeks or months in Betty's siege.

When the siege ends, Betty's group returns to base and Robin and Usopp unexpectedly collide there. They can later take the same route back toward Sabaody without spending the whole timeskip attached at the hip.
