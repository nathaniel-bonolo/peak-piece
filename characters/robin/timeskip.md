# Robin: Timeskip

## Arrival

Kuma sends Robin directly to a Revolutionary Army base.

Peak Piece removes the need for:

> Robin is sent into another slave-labor nightmare -> the RA happens to liberate the location -> they finally take her to the faction that already wanted her.

Kuma already knows the Revolutionary Army.

The direct route is cleaner.

## Initial relationship

The RA should not treat Robin as a mystical second Chosen One.

They value her for concrete reasons:

- Ohara's surviving scholarship
- Poneglyph literacy
- historical reconstruction
- World Government knowledge
- intelligence value
- experience surviving state pursuit

Robin is useful because of what she knows and what she can do.

## Two years

After Luffy's message creates the two-year window, Robin chooses to remain and learn.

Her curriculum should expand her existing specialty rather than replace it.

Likely areas:

- intelligence systems
- archives
- propaganda analysis
- secure information handling
- clandestine networks
- political geography
- historical logistics
- how revolutionary cells preserve evidence under repression

Exact curriculum is still open.

## Usopp

[[characters/usopp/timeskip|Usopp]] can also spend the timeskip with the Revolutionary Army.

They do not need the same experience.

Robin belongs closer to strategic information and history.

Usopp belongs closer to field operations and asymmetric warfare.
