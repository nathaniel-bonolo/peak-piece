# Nico Robin: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Robin's pre-timeskip character arc peaks at Enies Lobby with "I want to live." Kuma later sends her to Tequila Wolf, where Revolutionaries eventually free/contact her before she joins the RA for the timeskip.

## What canon appears to be doing

Enies Lobby resolves Robin's belief that her existence is a burden. The RA is a natural political/historical destination for the survivor of Ohara.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Enies Lobby can appear to finish her development too early; Tequila Wolf repeats victimization before delivering her to a faction Kuma already knows; her happier post-timeskip personality receives little causal development; archaeology is underused as active intelligence; "Light of the Revolution" risks chosen-person framing.

## Peak Piece response

Kuma sends Robin directly to the RA base. Enies Lobby answers "do I want to live?" The timeskip asks "who am I now that I am allowed to?" Choice, scholarship, adult peers, archives, intelligence work, and small social moments make her later happiness causal.

## Why this solution

Robin does not need another giant trauma. The strongest next step is learning what she chooses when survival and usefulness are no longer the only questions.

## What we preserve

Enies Lobby, Ohara, Poneglyph literacy, reserve, dry/morbid humor, archaeology, intelligence instincts, and the RA connection.

## What we intentionally reject

Tequila Wolf as required suffering and chosen-one-like revolutionary framing.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make the RA a magical therapy camp or turn Robin into a generic spy. Preserve the archaeologist and straight man.
