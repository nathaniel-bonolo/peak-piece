# Whitebeard: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Whitebeard protects Fish-Man Island by placing it under his name and is deeply respected by Jinbe.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses an Emperor's flag as deterrence and Whitebeard's chosen-family piracy as an alternative model of power.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Protection can remain abstract if the story never specifies what changes for ordinary Fishmen because Whitebeard exists.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Whitebeard protects people and movement: he frees captured Fishmen, makes trafficking expensive, protects routes, and lets Fishmen travel/trade without becoming merchandise. After he dies, the roads close again.

## Why this solution

This converts reputation into everyday political consequence and grounds Jinbe's loyalty.

## What we preserve

Chosen family, deterrence, Fish-Man Island protection, Jinbe connection, Emperor status.

## What we intentionally reject

Protection as only a flag on a map.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make Whitebeard a flawless institution. His system is fragile because it depends heavily on him personally.
