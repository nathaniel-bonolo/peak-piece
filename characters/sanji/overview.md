# Sanji

Status: Draft

## Current direction

Whole Cake Island is being rebuilt without requiring the Germa backstory.

Sanji should matter because of who he already is:

- elite cook
- combatant
- tactician
- crew logistics specialist
- someone whose absence creates material problems for the Straw Hats

His character should not be held together by pervert gags.

## WCI capture direction

Current proposal direction:

Charlotte intelligence evaluates the Straw Hats.

Luffy has enormous future value and is worth allowing to grow.

Sanji has high current value, is more isolatable, and removing him damages the crew operationally.

He can therefore be selected as a harvest target and bait for Luffy.

This is still under discussion.
