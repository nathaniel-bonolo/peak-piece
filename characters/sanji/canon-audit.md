# Sanji: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Sanji is cook, combatant, strategist, and later Vinsmoke prince. WCI is heavily tied to Germa and arranged marriage; his canon timeskip occurs on Kamabakka.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon combines food, masculinity, family abuse, romance, self-sacrifice, and chosen-vs-biological family.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Peak Piece wants his cooking to do more causal work. Germa ancestry should not be required to make him plot-important, and a timeskip destination should develop his actual specialization.

## Peak Piece response

Current direction sends Sanji toward a food/cuisine culture that expands nutrition, preservation, logistics, rare ingredients, recovery food, and feeding a crew under extreme conditions. WCI targets him for operational value and leverage rather than requiring the Germa marriage machine.

## Why this solution

Sanji is most distinctive when cooking is plot-relevant expertise, not flavor between fights.

## What we preserve

Cooking, emotional sensitivity, strategy, food ethics, combat, and crew survival value.

## What we intentionally reject

A theme-first timeskip destination and Germa as mandatory explanation for his importance.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not turn Sanji into a nutrition spreadsheet. Food must remain sensory, emotional, funny, and personal.

## WCI emotional replacement

Removing Germa creates a real obligation.

"Sanji is the optimal hostage" is only operational logic.

Peak WCI still needs a Sanji-specific wound.

Current answer:

> **Sanji loves through usefulness and can therefore treat his own needs as cheaper than everybody else's.**

This preserves a personal self-sacrifice arc without requiring biological-family machinery to make Sanji matter.

## Failure mode

Do not let "the crew needs the cook" become the emotional answer.

The point is that they want **Sanji**, not only the services Sanji performs.
