# Boa Hancock: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Hancock is Kuja empress, a Conqueror's Haki user, and overwhelmingly infatuated with Luffy after Amazon Lily.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses the infatuation for comedy while making her a crucial ally during the Ace/Marineford crisis.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Her love can remain mostly a marriage gag while Luffy's serious two-year Haki development is outsourced to Rayleigh despite Amazon Lily being a Haki culture.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Boa turns infatuation into genuine concern after Marineford. She organizes Luffy's training through Kuja specialists and personally handles Conqueror's Haki.

## Why this solution

Boa should act like someone who desperately wants Luffy alive while preserving the absurd comedy of how she interprets him.

## What we preserve

Infatuation, arrogance, beauty, Kuja authority, Haki, comedy, loyalty.

## What we intentionally reject

Love that only produces fantasy gags and no long-term action.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not flatten Boa into a healthy supportive girlfriend. Keep pride, absurdity, politics, and overinterpretation.
