# Boa Hancock

## Luffy

Boa is not mildly fond of Luffy.

She is utterly infatuated with him.

Peak Piece keeps that comedy while allowing the emotion to produce sensible action.

## After Marineford

Boa sees what Marineford proves:

Luffy survived encounters far above his level because stronger people repeatedly intervened.

She has no intention of letting him sail back into the New World like that.

She organizes a serious Haki curriculum using Amazon Lily's specialists.

## Training role

Boa does **not** need to teach every branch herself.

She recruits the people best suited to teach:

- Observation applications
- Armament applications
- efficiency and control
- weapon integration

Boa personally trains Luffy in Conqueror's Haki because she possesses it and is well positioned to teach it.

## Amazon Lily rules

Boa helping Luffy does not mean the island suddenly abandons its rules.

Luffy remains outside the city and trains on the outskirts.

## Open

A future Boa/Luffy romance or marriage is not canon yet.
