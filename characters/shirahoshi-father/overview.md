# Shirahoshi's Father

Name: **TBD**

## Core

Shirahoshi's father was a Gem researcher associated with Fisher Tiger's crew.

He studied old Fishman Gem techniques and helped recover knowledge of [[systems/gem-injection|Gem Injection]].

## Tiger

His research contributes to Tiger being able to use the old technique during the liberation struggle.

Tiger's later medical collapse proves how dangerous the technique can become when overused.

## Shirahoshi

Shirahoshi grows up around the research.

She repeatedly enters her father's study and eventually finds the technique herself.

That means her later knowledge is inherited through study, not destiny or royal blood.
