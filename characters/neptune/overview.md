# King Neptune

## Core

Neptune rules a country that sits on one of the world's most important Gem basins.

His job is not ceremonial.

He must manage:

- extraction
- trade
- trafficking
- surface access
- Emperor pressure
- World Government pressure
- internal resistance
- food and shipping constraints
- public fear

## After Whitebeard

[[characters/whitebeard/overview|Whitebeard]] gave Fishmen safe movement through deterrence.

When Whitebeard dies, that protection collapses.

Kidnapping, trafficking, and unsafe surface movement rapidly return.

Neptune faces a real emergency.

## Linlin deal

[[characters/linlin/overview|Linlin]] offers stability.

In return she receives:

- control over major Gem extraction
- a required worker quota
- a large share of output
- a required number of Fishman "residents" in Totto Land

Those residents function as hostages whether Linlin uses that word or not.

The mines receive basic conditions and a minimal share of profit.

Neptune accepts because the alternative may immediately cost lives.

## Moral position

The rewrite should not make Neptune stupid or cowardly merely so Shirahoshi can be right.

Shirahoshi can correctly call the deal slavery with extra steps.

Neptune can correctly remember how many people disappeared when the roads became unsafe.

That conflict is stronger if both understand the cost.
