# Neptune: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Neptune is Fish-Man Island's king and Shirahoshi's father, operating under external pirate pressure and Otohime's legacy.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses the royal family as institutional leadership caught between coexistence ideals and backlash.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Peak Piece removes Shirahoshi's princess status and needs Neptune to make a hard rational choice rather than exist around a chosen-princess plot.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Neptune accepts Linlin's coercive protection/extraction agreement because he has counted the kidnappings, unsafe routes, and disappearances likely if he refuses. Shirahoshi can hate the decision while the audience understands why he made it.

## Why this solution

A competent desperate ruler creates a better political conflict than a foolish ruler who exists to be corrected.

## What we preserve

Institutional leadership, coexistence pressure, concern for survival, burden of ruling under stronger powers.

## What we intentionally reject

Royal chosen-one structure around Shirahoshi.

## Failure modes to watch

Compromise must genuinely save some lives and genuinely harm others. Do not make Neptune secretly right about everything.
