# King Neptune: Progression

Neptune's arc should test the line between:

- compromise that saves lives
- compromise that becomes collaboration
- temporary dependence
- surrender of sovereignty

The Straw Hats should not simply prove he was an idiot.

They should create a new option that did not previously exist.
