# Kaido: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Kaido builds the Beast Pirates around Zoans and SMILEs, occupies Wano, runs weapons production, possesses overwhelming power/Haki, and later states that Haki can transcend all.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon emphasizes raw force, animal imagery, Wano oppression, war obsession, and overwhelming Emperor power.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

SMILE's poor success profile makes indiscriminate consumption strategically irrational; the Beast Pirates often read as boss tiers more than an army; Zoan obsession and "Haki transcends all" framing can pull against each other; Wano isolation sits awkwardly beside the regime's global trade/tech needs.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Kaido is a military pragmatist. Natural Zoans prove specialized bodies are useful; SMILE tries to industrialize that advantage. Caesar invents, Queen classifies/militarizes, Kaido sets strategy. Wano's people are isolated while the regime imports whatever strengthens the war machine.

## Why this solution

The Nobunaga structural reference gives Kaido a coherent way to adopt useful military technology without historical cosplay.

## What we preserve

Dragon terror, raw strength, war obsession, Wano industry, Zoan army aesthetic, brutality, and home-field dominance.

## What we intentionally reject

Randomly nerfing most of the army through SMILE and using Haki rhetoric to invalidate the military project he built.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make Kaido a genius at everything. Queen and commanders need real jobs. Keep the philosophy simple and character-consistent.
