# Charlotte Genauche

## Core

Charlotte Genauche is Linlin's daughter and the governor/administrator responsible for the Fishman Island Gem operation.

Her Devil Fruit and personality make her exceptionally suited to mining.

She is not a cackling sadist.

She genuinely believes she **modernized** an extraction system that was previously chaotic, violent, and inefficient.

## Worldview

Before Charlotte control, Fishmen were kidnapped, privately sold, tortured, worked to death, and moved through fragmented illegal systems.

Genauche introduces order:

- regulated shifts
- basic working conditions
- food
- medical treatment
- safer tunnels
- controlled output
- technical wages or profit shares
- lower random mortality
- fewer arbitrary beatings

None of that makes the workers free.

Genauche's moral blind spot is that she treats improvement inside coercion as proof that the coercion is legitimate.

When Shirahoshi calls the arrangement slavery, Genauche can sincerely believe that description ignores everything she improved.

## Hordy

Genauche sees [[characters/hordy/overview|Hordy]] as evidence that useful Fishmen can rise under her administration.

She trusts him with mine culture, discipline, and labor management.

She does not understand that giving a prisoner a supervisory title does not make him free.

## Function in the arc

Genauche embodies:

> **exploitation made efficient, technical, and respectable**

This separates her from random cruelty.
