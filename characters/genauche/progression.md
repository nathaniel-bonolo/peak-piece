# Charlotte Genauche: Progression

Genauche begins the arc confident that the Charlotte system is materially better than what came before.

The rebellion forces her to confront a distinction she does not naturally make:

> **better conditions are not the same thing as consent**

She does not need to become good or renounce Linlin.

Her job is to remain a competent antagonist whose best argument is still insufficient.
