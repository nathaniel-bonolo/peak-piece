# Hordy: Relationships

## Charlotte Genauche

Genauche sees Hordy as proof that her mine hierarchy can reward useful Fishmen.

Hordy sees her as the person who gave him a better position inside the same cage.

## Shirahoshi

Shirahoshi despises what Hordy became inside the mine.

Hordy considers her rebellion strategically useful but potentially reckless.

Both want Linlin's extraction regime gone.

They disagree about methods, sacrifice, and Gem injection.

## Fisher Tiger

Tiger is proof, to Hordy, that Gem injection can create enough power to change history.

Shirahoshi sees the same history as proof of the technique's cost.

## The miners

Hordy is simultaneously:

- one of them
- their overseer
- their abuser
- sometimes the person secretly reducing a punishment
- the man many of them understandably hate most
