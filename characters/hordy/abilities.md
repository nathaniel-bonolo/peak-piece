# Hordy: Abilities

## Fishman physiology

Hordy is naturally physically powerful and adapted to underwater combat.

## Sea-creature handling

Like other Fishmen, Hordy has some ability to work with and tame sea creatures.

This exists on a spectrum across Fishmen.

He is obsessed with the legendary possibility of becoming strong or skilled enough to tame an actual Sea King.

## Gem injection

Hordy intends to use [[systems/gem-injection|Gem Injection]] despite knowing that miners have died attempting crude versions.

The exact mechanics and final combat use remain under development.
