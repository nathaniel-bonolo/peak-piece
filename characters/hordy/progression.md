# Hordy: Progression

## Childhood belief

Hordy believes strength will eventually let someone save the miners.

When nobody comes, he begins to believe he must become strong enough himself.

## Collaboration

Collective punishment teaches him that direct resistance can multiply suffering.

He becomes the person holding the whip because he believes he can minimize the damage.

This makes him safer, more powerful, and more hated.

## Genauche

Hordy's rise under [[characters/genauche/overview|Genauche]] is deliberately humiliating.

He reaches almost as high as the extraction system allows a useful Fishman to rise and discovers that the ceiling is still beneath Charlotte authority.

## Shirahoshi deal

[[characters/shirahoshi/overview|Shirahoshi]] is imprisoned after repeatedly resisting Linlin's collection system.

Hordy wants access to her father's knowledge of [[systems/gem-injection|Gem Injection]].

They make a temporary deal:

- Hordy needs her rebellion and knowledge
- Shirahoshi needs his keys and access

They do not need to trust or like one another.

## Pen ritual

At home Hordy keeps pen, paper, alcohol, and a private apology ritual.

He writes to people he hurt, burns the letter, then showers so nobody sees him cry.

The ritual establishes that his guilt is not theoretical.

## Ending direction

The right hand Hordy used to write and whip is eventually lost to a Sea King.

He returns home by muscle memory and reaches for the pen with a hand that is no longer there.

He writes one last apology with his left hand.

This time he does not burn it.

The shower water eventually washes the ink away.

He dies having finally stopped trying to justify the Devil's Hand.

Exact staging remains subject to chapter work.
