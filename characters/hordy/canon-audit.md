# Hordy Jones: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Hordy is deliberately revealed as someone whose hatred is not rooted in a personal atrocity committed against him; the Fish-Man District environment transmits hatred to him.

## What canon appears to be doing

The canon villain embodies inherited hatred reproduced socially even when the inheritor did not personally experience the originating grievance.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Peak Piece finds that function intellectually clear but emotionally and causally less rich than the alternative. Canon Hordy can feel more like a thesis delivery mechanism than a contradictory person.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Hordy is a former child miner/slave who survives by becoming useful to the oppressive system, takes the whip to reduce collective punishment, becomes a Fishman kapo, rationalizes violence as harm reduction, and eventually tries to seize Gem injection as liberation power.

## Why this solution

The rewrite studies what oppression does to someone who starts using its violence as a survival strategy.

## What we preserve

Fishman anger, generational pressure, extremism, the danger of hatred, and a villain whose actions threaten liberation.

## What we intentionally reject

Hordy as the pure "nothing happened personally to me" embodiment. We understand the canon thesis and deliberately choose another.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make trauma automatically justify him. His reasoning can be understandable while his violence remains destructive.
