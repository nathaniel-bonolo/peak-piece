# Hordy Jones

## Core

Hordy is a Fishman who was kidnapped as a child and forced into Gem mining.

He grows up hearing two stories:

1. one day a "Sea King" or Poseidon figure strong enough to tame an actual Sea King might save them
2. an ancient Gem technique can make a Fishman enormously stronger

He becomes convinced that he may need to become that savior himself.

## The mine

Hordy watches friends attempt crude Gem injection and die.

He also learns that rebellion is punished collectively.

When one miner finally attacks a human overseer and grabs a whip, Hordy takes the whip first and beats the Fishman himself because he knows the humans will punish everyone otherwise.

The overseers are impressed.

Hordy becomes a **kapo**, a Fishman used to police other Fishmen.

His reasoning is simple:

> If somebody is going to hold the whip, I can make it hurt less than they will.

## Devil's Hand

Among the miners he becomes known as **the Devil's Hand**.

[[characters/genauche/overview|Charlotte Genauche]] eventually makes him her most important Fishman subordinate.

He supervises labor, translates mine culture into administrative information, identifies unrest, and keeps production stable.

He is useful.

He is never truly equal.

## Contradiction

Hordy can believe all of these things at once:

- the system is evil
- open rebellion gets people killed
- his collaboration reduces suffering
- he is preparing for the day he can destroy the system
- the people he hurts should hate him

That contradiction is the character.
