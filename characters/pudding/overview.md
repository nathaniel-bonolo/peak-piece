# Charlotte Pudding

Status: Draft

## Core

Pudding is a memory specialist whose Fruit forces her to relive memories in first person.

She does not simply watch a recording.

If a soldier remembers being stabbed, Pudding remembers being that soldier and being stabbed.

If somebody remembers loving a daughter for twenty years, Pudding temporarily carries the emotional reality of that love.

Repeated extraction damages her sense of self.

## Identity problem

Pudding's danger is not "too many facts."

It is identity contamination.

Possible stages:

1. emotional residue
2. behavioral bleed
3. relationship bleed
4. identity substitution
5. displacement of her own autobiographical memory

## Journals

### Journal One: Who am I?

Pudding records her own life, habits, preferences, relationships, tiny memories, and identity anchors.

Family members may eventually contribute their memories of her as an external backup.

### Journal Two: Who have I carried?

Names and details of people whose memories she has extracted.

This prevents them from disappearing into anonymous intelligence packages.

## Ritual

Early:

> My name is Charlotte Pudding.  
> I am here voluntarily.  
> These memories are not mine.  
> I am Pudding when I enter. I will be Pudding when I return.

Mature direction:

> These memories are with me. They are not me.

Potential later line:

> I am Charlotte Pudding. I decide what I carry forward.
