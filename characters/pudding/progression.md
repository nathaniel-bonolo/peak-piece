# Charlotte Pudding: Progression

## Thesis

Early Pudding may implicitly believe:

> I am my memories.

That fails.

Her mature understanding becomes closer to:

> I am the person who chooses what to do with the memories I carry.

## Soul Feast

Soul Feast overloads an already established system.

The war should not introduce her entire identity problem from scratch.

The audience should already know:

- the journals
- the ritual
- behavioral bleed
- the cost of extraction

The war then pushes the system to failure.
