# Charlotte Brulee

## Mirror-Mirror Fruit

Brulee can enter and move through the Mirror World without Gems.

The Fruit itself is hers.

A Gem does not turn the ability on.

## Natural limits

Normal use is constrained by:

- Brulee's stamina
- concentration
- attuned mirror infrastructure
- range / operational horizon
- the number and stability of active crossings

## Military-scale use

Soul Feast pushes the Fruit far beyond ordinary personal transport.

Moving thousands of soldiers, equipment, supplies, and repeated crossings requires [[systems/gems|Gem]] support because Brulee's own energy budget is not enough to sustain that scale.

Gems extend output.

They do not remove the need for mirrors, attunement, range, or Brulee herself.
