# Charlotte Brulee: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Brulee's Mirror-Mirror Fruit grants access to the Mirro-World and movement through mirrors.

## What canon appears to be doing

The Fruit is a fairy-tale spatial tool, prison, infiltration route, and escape mechanic.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

At army scale, unrestricted mirror transport can erase distance, naval logistics, and defensive geography.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Brulee uses her Fruit normally with her own stamina. Gems only support absurd military scale: thousands of people/equipment and repeated sustained crossings. Mirrors, range, attunement, infrastructure, and Brulee remain constraints.

## Why this solution

The Fruit stays a Devil Fruit rather than becoming Gem-powered magic while large-scale warfare gains real costs.

## What we preserve

Mirror World weirdness, fairy-tale imagery, infiltration, escape, Brulee as critical operator.

## What we intentionally reject

The stale idea that every crossing needs Gems.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not explain every throughput constraint in dialogue. Show exhaustion, bottlenecks, damaged mirrors, and consequences.
