# Marines

## Haki

Before Marineford, Haki knowledge is restricted and uneven.

After Marineford makes Haki public, the Marines must expand training to stay competitive.

## Technology

The Marines use expensive weapon platforms like Pacifistas when preparation, logistics, and strategic value justify them.

They do not have infinite deployment.
