# Helping Pudding Remember

Pudding starts losing certainty about which memories are hers.

The family has routines.

One sibling brings her journal. Another reminds her of a stupid childhood event. Somebody corrects a detail. Someone else remembers something she forgot.

The scene can be mundane. That is the point.

Her identity is not held together only by dramatic speeches. Her family helps hold the thread with her.
