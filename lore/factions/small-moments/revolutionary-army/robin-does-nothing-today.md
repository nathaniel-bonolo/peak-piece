# Robin Does Nothing Today

Robin has no assigned work and asks whether somebody forgot to give her a task.

They tell her no. She can read, sleep, eat, walk, or do nothing.

Robin points out that she is not injured.

They know.

The scene's job is to make ordinary rest feel strange to somebody who spent years treating usefulness as rent.
