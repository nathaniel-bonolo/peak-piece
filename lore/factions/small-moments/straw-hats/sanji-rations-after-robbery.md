# Sanji Leaves Rations

The Straw Hats rob a pirate crew that refused Luffy's fleet offer.

Nami takes treasure. Usopp takes useful ammunition. Sanji finds a rare ingredient. Luffy steals the meat they were cooking because of course he does.

Then Sanji checks the galley.

If the crew is genuinely low on food, he quietly leaves enough rations that the robbery will not make them starve.

No moral speech. Sanji is a cook. That is the line.
