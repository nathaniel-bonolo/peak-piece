# Robin the Zoologist

The crew starts some incomprehensible group stupidity.

Robin declines, then sits nearby watching with genuine curiosity like a zoologist observing an undiscovered species.

Happiness does not require her to become as childish as the younger crew. The straight-man role survives; the affection underneath it grows.
