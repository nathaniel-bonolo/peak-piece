# Small Moments

Large emotional payoffs need ordinary life underneath them.

These notes collect faction moments that may only take a panel, a page, or a short scene. The point is accumulated meaning: affection, habit, friction, trust, routine, family structure, and private contradiction without requiring a giant speech.
