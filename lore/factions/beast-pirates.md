# Beast Pirates

## Strategic identity

The Beast Pirates are not just:

> Kaido + dinosaurs + a sequence of boss fights.

Their core military idea is to collect, manufacture, and organize unusual physical advantages.

[[characters/kaido/overview|Kaido]] identifies the strategic direction.

[[characters/queen/overview|Queen]] turns biological outcomes into doctrine.

## Natural Zoans

Kaido actively hunts, recruits, buys, and concentrates natural Zoan users because he believes specialized bodies create military advantages conventional troops cannot easily reproduce.

Natural Zoans are valuable but scarce.

## SMILE program

Caesar initially tries to create faithful artificial Zoans.

The early results include:

- permanent animal traits
- emotional abnormalities
- pain abnormalities
- aggression
- unstable body plans
- outright debilitating failures

Kaido changes the intention.

Queen begins classifying outcomes by battlefield usefulness rather than asking whether every result is a successful copy of a natural Zoan.

## Hyena doctrine

Useful abnormal traits can be selected into specialist groups.

The Hyena direction focuses on combinations such as:

- low fear
- abnormal pain response
- aggression
- willingness to keep fighting through injury

These troops become especially disruptive against the Charlotte casualty-recovery and fear-based soul economy.

## How ideas move through the organization

Kaido sets strategic appetite.

Queen turns mechanics into doctrine.

Other specialists contribute capabilities.

This distinction matters.

Apoo can be recruited because Queen sees what amplified sound enables.

Queen can revive Sky Coffin because Apoo changes the engineering problem.

Kaido can approve the cost without personally inventing either system.

> **Authority is not ideation.**

## Competence is not omniscience

The Beast Pirates prepare for weapons they know.

They still make assumptions.

They still waste money.

They still have abandoned projects.

They still discover new interactions during testing.

The old Sky Coffin program being cancelled at ten percent and later revived is a useful model:

a competent organization can make the correct decision to kill an uneconomic project and later make the correct decision to reopen it when technology changes.
