# Beast Pirates

## Zoan concentration

Kaido actively hunts, recruits, buys, and concentrates natural Zoan users.

## SMILE doctrine

Caesar initially tries to create faithful Zoan replication.

The early product produces permanent traits, personality defects, pain abnormalities, and aggression.

Kaido chooses to turn the bugs into the product.

Desired traits include:

- low fear
- abnormal pain response
- aggression
- resilience
- willingness to keep fighting through injury

This becomes the basis for Hyena-style shock troops.
