# Straw Hat Fleet

## Origin

Peak Piece does not hand Luffy a fleet because several people he saved suddenly decide to pledge their lives and soldiers to an impulsive pirate.

Luffy gradually chooses to build one.

This grows out of his changing understanding of piracy and power.

## Recruitment

Luffy can approach a crew he likes with the same stupid grin he has always had:

> Wanna join my fleet?

Joining is voluntary.

Refusing does not make the crew an enemy.

## What the flag means

Luffy is not interested in writing a giant legal code.

But sailing under his flag comes with basic lines:

- no slavery
- no trafficking
- no predatory attacks on civilians
- no using the Straw Hat name to extort weaker communities
- no treating Luffy's protection as permission to become somebody else's tyrant

The crews remain autonomous.

This is a fleet, not an empire built on taxes and governors.

## Still pirates

The Straw Hats do not become Marines.

A crew can refuse Luffy and still discover that Nami and Usopp stole their treasure while everybody was distracted by Luffy's speech.

Sanji may take rare ingredients.

Luffy is absolutely stealing meat if dinner is sitting there.

The distinction is that Sanji will not knowingly leave ordinary people or a crew without enough food to survive. If their stores are dangerously low, he may leave rations behind.

The Straw Hats can rob somebody without deliberately making them helpless.

## When bounties matter

Refusing Luffy is not enough.

Fighting back after being robbed is not enough.

The line is **predation**.

A crew becomes a serious bounty target when the Straw Hats learn they do things such as:

- slavery
- trafficking
- raiding civilians
- kidnapping for profit
- murdering surrendered crews
- extorting weak islands
- repeatedly preying on people who cannot resist them

Then [[characters/zoro/overview|Pirate Hunter Zoro]] becomes relevant again.

## Government bounties are not moral truth

The World Government puts bounties on people Luffy considers friends.

A wanted poster is evidence of legal status, not proof of guilt.

The crew judges the person first.

## Funding

Bounty collection can help fund:

- ship repairs
- food
- ammunition
- allied operations
- travel
- fleet logistics

The fleet does not need to fund itself by taxing islands under Luffy's protection.
