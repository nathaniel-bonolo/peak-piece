# Revolutionary Army: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline
Canon establishes Dragon's anti-World-Government organization, regional commanders, Kuma history, Sabo, Ivankov, Betty, Lindbergh, Karasu, Morley, and others.

## Peak opportunity
The RA is uniquely suited to become an actual school of intelligence, sabotage, propaganda, logistics, clandestine history, and asymmetric warfare rather than mostly a distant plot faction.

## Robin
Canon already connects Robin to the RA. Peak Piece removes the Tequila Wolf detour and deepens the reason the relationship matters.

## Usopp
Usopp joining is new. The justification is functional: a Revolutionary war zone teaches exactly the weak-vs-strong warfare his Water 7 duel tells him he needs.

## Betty
Canon Betty empowers civilians through encouragement. Peak Betty sharpens this into resolve amplification with agency as a hard limit.
