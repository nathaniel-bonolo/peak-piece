# Gem History

Status: Draft

## Ancient deposits

Natural Gem deposits once existed across a much larger accessible world.

## Sea-level rise

Roughly 800 years ago, the sea level rose dramatically.

Many major Gem deposits became submerged.

Current direction:

Imu and the World Government had already accumulated major stockpiles and the flood partly strengthened their Gem monopoly by denying challengers easy access to strategic energy.

This may not be the only motive for the flood.

## Fishmen

Fishmen became essential because they could access underwater deposits that surface powers could not mine as easily.

That made Fishman communities strategically valuable and vulnerable to coercion.

## Government control

The World Government's monopoly rests on more than owning mines.

It includes:

- stockpiles
- military power
- refining
- trade
- shipping
- legal control
- coercion
- information suppression
