# Fishman Liberation History

## Extraction era

Fishmen are strategically valuable because of their access to submerged Gem deposits.

Existing prejudice is amplified and exploited by governments, pirates, traffickers, and extraction interests.

Dehumanization makes coercive labor cheaper politically.

## Fisher Tiger

[[characters/fisher-tiger/overview|Fisher Tiger]] becomes a liberation figure through direct action.

A Gem researcher in his circle, [[characters/shirahoshi-father/overview|Shirahoshi's father]], helps recover an old Fishman technique for using Gems directly in the body.

Tiger uses and overuses it.

His later need for a blood transfusion becomes part of the technique's historical warning.

## Whitebeard

[[characters/whitebeard/overview|Whitebeard]] changes the political situation by extending deterrence to Fishmen as a people.

He makes surface movement safer, frees slaves, helps return kidnapped Fishmen, and protects fairer Gem trade.

## Collapse

Whitebeard's death at Marineford creates a power vacuum.

The return of trafficking and unsafe routes makes Linlin's later protection deal politically possible.

## Linlin

Linlin restores security while converting protection into extraction, quotas, and hostage leverage.

This is the system the Straw Hats encounter during the Fishman Island arc.
