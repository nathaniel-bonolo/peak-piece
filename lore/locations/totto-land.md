# Totto Land

Status: Draft

## Terminology

Totto Land is the archipelago.

Whole Cake Island is one island within Totto Land and also the name commonly used for the arc.

Do not use the two names interchangeably when geography matters.

## Function

Peak Totto Land should feel like the center of a centuries-old imperial family state.

It should have:

- civilian life
- food production
- military infrastructure
- soul economy
- Gem logistics
- trade
- transport
- intelligence
- internal family politics

Not every citizen should exist only to sing about candy.
