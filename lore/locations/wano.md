# Wano

## Isolation for whom?

Peak Kaido does not need to believe in sakoku.

Wano can be closed to ordinary people while Kaido's regime freely interacts with the outside world whenever doing so strengthens the regime.

The population gets isolation.

Kaido gets access to:

- weapons markets
- scientists
- technology
- specialists
- Devil Fruits
- foreign intelligence
- exports and imports

The country is isolated.

The regime is not.

## Military-industrial state

Kaido uses Wano as a long-term military base.

Its:

- factories
- terrain
- workforce
- weapons production
- defensible geography
- imported expertise

feed the army he is building.

Oppression therefore has material purpose rather than existing only as villain atmosphere.

## Soul Feast

The Soul Feast War does **not** happen as a canon Onigashima-style raid.

Wano itself is Kaido's prepared home battlefield.

He has had years to shape:

- the basin
- mountains
- speaker infrastructure
- traps
- fortifications
- floating terrain
- supply routes

General Linlin can arrive with a competent expeditionary plan and still face tools she could not reasonably know.

## Historical design influence

See [[references/history/oda-nobunaga|Oda Nobunaga]].

The useful structure is military pragmatism and selective openness, not copying a historical biography beat for beat.
