# Wano

## Isolation for whom?

Peak Kaido does not need to believe in sakoku.

Wano can be closed to ordinary people while Kaido's regime freely interacts with the outside world whenever doing so strengthens the regime.

The population gets isolation.

Kaido gets access to:

- weapons markets
- scientists
- technology
- specialists
- Devil Fruits
- foreign intelligence
- exports and imports

The country is isolated.

The regime is not.

## Military-industrial state

Kaido uses Wano as a long-term military base.

Its:

- factories
- terrain
- workforce
- weapons production
- defensible geography
- imported expertise

feed the army he is building.

Oppression therefore has material purpose rather than existing only as villain atmosphere.

## Reflective-surface denial

Household mirrors are unusually rare under Orochi.

The public justification can be ridiculous:

> mirrors encourage vanity

or another self-important morality claim.

Locals can mock it.

A child saying Orochi simply does not want to see his own ugly face is enough to sell the scene.

### Actual reason

The policy originates with Kaido.

He knows [[characters/brulee/overview|Brulee]] and her Mirror World are one of Linlin's nastiest infiltration / deployment tools.

Brulee cannot use any random reflection as a free portal.

She still needs usable / attuned mirror infrastructure.

Kaido's reasoning is simpler:

> **Why leave convenient candidates everywhere?**

So the regime suppresses:

- household mirrors
- large clean decorative mirrors
- unnecessarily reflective interior surfaces where practical

The ban does not need to be perfect.

Water still reflects.

Metal still shines.

The point is reducing convenient infrastructure, not abolishing reflection.

The story can present this only as a weird Wano law and never explicitly explain the strategic reason.

See [[story/sagas/soul-feast/arcs/soul-feast/scene-seeds|Soul Feast Scene Seeds]].

## Soul Feast

The Soul Feast War does **not** happen as a canon Onigashima-style raid.

Wano itself is Kaido's prepared home battlefield.

He has had years to shape:

- the basin
- mountains
- speaker infrastructure
- traps
- fortifications
- floating terrain
- supply routes

General Linlin can arrive with a competent expeditionary plan and still face tools she could not reasonably know.

## Historical design influence
