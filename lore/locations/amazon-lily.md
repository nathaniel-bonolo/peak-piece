# Amazon Lily

## Haki culture

Amazon Lily is one of the surviving cultures where Haki is not merely elite trivia.

It is integrated into martial life.

Different Kuja specialize in different applications rather than every warrior being equally good at every branch.

## Luffy's training

After Marineford, [[characters/boa-hancock/overview|Boa Hancock]] arranges Luffy's Haki education here.

Luffy does not get permission to casually live inside the city.

Training happens on the outskirts with Kuja teachers coming to him.

## Rusukaina

The dangerous island used in canon remains useful as an application ground.

Training loop:

1. learn a technique
2. test it under pressure in the jungle
3. discover what was misunderstood
4. return to the Kuja
5. get corrected
6. repeat

The teachers provide discipline.

The island tests whether Luffy can actually use it.
