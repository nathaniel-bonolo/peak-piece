# Gem Economy

See [[systems/gems|Gems]] for the energy mechanic.

This file focuses on markets, extraction, trade, monopoly, and politics.

## Strategic role

Gems function like a combination of:

- oil
- batteries
- diamonds
- military reserve currency

They are portable, finite, valuable, and required for many extreme applications.

## Fishman Island

[[lore/locations/fishman-island|Fishman Island]] sits on one of the world's most important Gem basins.

Its strategic power comes from both the resource and Fishmen's unique ability to reach submerged deposits.

## Whitebeard model

Whitebeard helps Fishman Island move and sell Gems while splitting profits fairly and protecting trade.

## Linlin model

Linlin provides security in exchange for extraction control, labor quotas, hostage leverage, and a dominant share of output.

This is not random villain taxation.

It is a strategic attempt to secure energy before major military and soul-economy commitments such as the Tea Party.

## Key principle

Gems create:

- strategic chokepoints
- trade dependencies
- imperial coercion
- black markets
- military budgets
- shipping targets
- embargoes
- resource diplomacy
