# Slavery and Extraction

Peak Piece treats exploitation as a functioning system, not only as cartoon cruelty.

## Fishmen

Fishmen are valuable because many can work underwater Gem deposits that ordinary humans cannot safely access.

That makes kidnapping economically attractive.

## Prejudice

Anti-Fishman prejudice does not need one single origin.

Physical difference, fear, historical separation, and conflict can produce prejudice organically.

The World Government and other extraction interests then **amplify and institutionalize** it because dehumanization makes exploitation easier.

Tools can include:

- propaganda
- legal classification
- controlled trade
- debt
- protection contracts
- quotas
- family hostages
- military pressure
- slavery
- criminal trafficking

## "Slavery with paperwork"

Linlin's Fishman Island agreement is deliberately more sophisticated than a whip-and-chain mine.

The workers can receive food, medical care, shifts, and limited pay.

The state can still be coercive if people cannot refuse the labor arrangement or if family members are held as leverage.

Improved conditions do not automatically create consent.
