# Slavery and Extraction

Status: Draft

Peak Piece should treat exploitation as a functioning system, not only as cartoon cruelty.

Possible tools of coercion include:

- legal dependency
- protection contracts
- debt
- quotas
- trade control
- hostage-taking
- family separation
- military occupation
- propaganda
- controlled shipping

Fishman exploitation should be connected to the world's need for underwater Gem extraction.
