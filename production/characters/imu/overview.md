# Imu

## Core

Peak Imu's power is built around deferred consequence and contracts.

He is not immortal.

He survives by making consequences wait and by finding somebody else to pay them.

## Personality

The mechanics should characterize him.

Imu is a man who avoids costs and makes others pay.

Progress against him means forcing a consequence to remain his.
