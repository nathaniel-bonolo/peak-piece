# Imu: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Imu is the hidden sovereign at the center of the World Government and is tied to erased history, ancient continuity, and extraordinary endgame power.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses Imu as the concealed personification of the contradiction beneath the Government's public structure.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Peak Piece wants the supernatural toolkit to express one coherent character idea rather than accumulate unrelated endgame powers.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Imu's core is deferred consequence: avoid costs now, transfer them through valid contracts/souls, and force somebody else to pay later. Linlin's contract is one expression.

## Why this solution

The power mirrors the character: Imu survives by making consequences belong to other people.

## What we preserve

Hidden sovereignty, ancient continuity, institutional control, fear of the forbidden past, endgame status.

## What we intentionally reject

An unrelated bundle of endgame abilities.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not overdefine Imu too early. Contract limits stay open until the endgame is ready.

## Linlin connection test

The Linlin relationship must remain causal rather than connective fanservice.

Remove a bad lore connection and almost nothing changes.

Remove this contract and major questions reopen:

- why does the World Government tolerate a four-century pirate empire?
- what does Linlin owe for that immunity?
- why does Imu accept her continued expansion?
- how does an external soul obligation reshape an economy Linlin already wants?

The contract passes the connection test only as long as Linlin remains the author of her own empire.

## Failure mode

Do not centralize every strange thing in the world under Imu.

If Linlin's culture, Tea Parties, family, soul use, or imperial identity would disappear without him, the relationship has swallowed too much.
