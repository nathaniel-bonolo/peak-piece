# Shirahoshi's Father: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

This Peak Piece role is largely original because Peak Shirahoshi is no longer Neptune's royal daughter.

## What canon appears to be doing

The role connects Gem research, Fisher Tiger, parental fear, and Shirahoshi's self-training.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Shirahoshi needs a parent whose refusal to train her is understandable rather than arbitrary.

## Peak Piece response

Her father is a Gem researcher tied to Fisher Tiger. He refuses because he understands the cost of violence and does not want his daughter turned into a weapon. His work later becomes part of her Gem-injection knowledge.

## Why this solution

The refusal creates friction while preserving love and giving Shirahoshi a reason to train secretly.

## What we preserve

A parent trying to keep a child safe in a dangerous political world.

## What we intentionally reject

Royal destiny parentage.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make him merely obstructive. His fear should be supported by what he witnessed.
