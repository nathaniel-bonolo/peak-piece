# Fisher Tiger: Progression

Tiger's story should preserve the tension between justified hatred and what he chooses to pass forward.

He does not have to forgive humans.

The important question is whether the next generation owes him his hatred.

Gem injection adds another layer to that legacy:

Tiger's body becomes one of the prices paid for liberation.
