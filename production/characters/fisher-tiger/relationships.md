# Fisher Tiger: Relationships

## Shirahoshi

Mentor figure and warrior influence.

## Shirahoshi's father

Research collaborator whose Gem work helps recover the dangerous old injection technique.

## Hordy

Hordy grows up on stories of Tiger and interprets Tiger's sacrifice as evidence that liberation justifies extreme self-destruction.

## Jinbe

Tiger is one of the foundational political and emotional influences on Jinbe's later search for durable Fishman security.
