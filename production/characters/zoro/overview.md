# Roronoa Zoro

## Pirate Hunter still matters

Peak Piece does not treat "Pirate Hunter" as a title that becomes meaningless once Zoro joins a pirate crew.

The Straw Hats can still turn predatory pirates in for bounties when doing so fits their judgment.

Zoro's old profession becomes useful to the growing [[production/lore/factions/straw-hat-fleet|Straw Hat Fleet]].

Bounty money can help fund:

- supplies
- repairs
- travel
- allied operations

## Important limit

A Government bounty is not proof somebody deserves capture.

Robin alone makes that obvious.

Zoro and the crew judge what the target actually does.
