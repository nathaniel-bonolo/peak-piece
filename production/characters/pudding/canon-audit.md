# Charlotte Pudding: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Pudding possesses the Memo-Memo Fruit, edits / extracts memories, has deep insecurity around her third eye, and shifts between hostility and affection during WCI.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses memory editing, identity performance, family pressure, and the conflict between performed and private selves.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

The psychological cost of repeatedly reliving other people's memories is underdeveloped relative to how invasive the power is.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Pudding relives memories first-person.

One person's coherent memory network is normally manageable.

Repeated extraction across **multiple people** creates the major identity risk because foreign emotional associations begin cross-linking.

Drift therefore includes ordinary residue as well as trauma:

- affection
- fear
- habits
- recognition
- homesickness
- relationship impulses

She develops:

- a simple pre-extraction mundane anchor
- Journal One, built by herself and family
- Journal Two, recording people she has carried
- military memory-curation skills
- an eventual distinction between a real feeling and a feeling that originated with Pudding

## Charlotte harvest role

Pudding's fear extraction now has an institutional purpose inside the soul system.

She helps identify routes that will make a high-value captive surrender to Linlin **without bruising the coherence that makes the soul valuable**.

Empathy does not automatically erase allegiance.

That moral contradiction is part of the character.

## Why this solution

A memory specialist should eventually struggle with the border between memories she carries and the self carrying them.

The danger should follow the actual structure of the power rather than behave like arbitrary amnesia.

## What we preserve

- Memo-Memo Fruit
- family loyalty
- insecurity
- intelligence
- emotional contradiction
- tenderness

## What we intentionally reject

- instant moral conversion from empathic exposure
- memory work as consequence-free information theft
- random identity loss with no causal pattern

## Failure modes to watch

Do not:

- make every extraction trigger a breakdown
- make one deep memory automatically dangerous if it is still one coherent life
- make every foreign emotion traumatic
- use the journals as magical cures
- overuse one comedy gag as Pudding's universal identity anchor
- turn her coping methods into clinical exposition in every scene

## Institutional imperfection

Pudding's military usefulness does not make her a flawless production machine.

Routine can create mistakes.

A harmless template defect can survive for years because repair costs more than tolerance.
