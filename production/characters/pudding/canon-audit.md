# Charlotte Pudding: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Pudding possesses the Memo-Memo Fruit, edits/extracts memories, has deep insecurity around her third eye, and shifts between hostility and affection during WCI.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses memory editing, identity performance, family pressure, and the conflict between performed and private selves.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

The psychological cost of repeatedly reliving other people's memories is underdeveloped relative to how invasive the power is.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Pudding relives memories first-person and risks identity contamination. She develops journals, rituals, family anchors, and military memory-curation skills. Empathy does not automatically erase allegiance.

## Why this solution

A memory specialist should eventually struggle with the border between memories she carries and the self carrying them.

## What we preserve

Memo-Memo Fruit, family loyalty, insecurity, intelligence, emotional contradiction, tenderness.

## What we intentionally reject

Instant moral conversion from empathic exposure.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make identity contamination random amnesia. Keep stages, coping tools, and agency.

## Institutional imperfection

Do not make Pudding's military usefulness mean she becomes a flawless production machine.

Routine can create mistakes.

A harmless template defect can survive for years because repair costs more than tolerance.

That kind of comedy pushes against sterile "optimized empire" writing while remaining causal.
