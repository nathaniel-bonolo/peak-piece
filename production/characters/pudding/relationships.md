# Charlotte Pudding: Relationships

## Linlin

Pudding chooses her family even while understanding their cruelty.

More information does not automatically make her morally certain.

Linlin is one of Pudding's strongest identity anchors because she remembers Pudding from long before Pudding began carrying other people's lives.

Linlin's love is real.

So is Linlin's willingness to use Pudding's ability inside a monstrous soul-harvesting system.

Those truths are allowed to coexist.

### After a bad extraction

When Pudding is badly drifted after carrying a captured pirate crew, Linlin later sits with her and tells her stories about **Pudding**:

- what she was like as a baby
- things she hated
- stupid childhood incidents
- family memories only somebody who knew her could provide

One locked story:

Pudding once hid so well during hide-and-seek that Katakuri dispatched **three search parties** because he thought something had happened to her.

Pudding begins to remember it while Linlin talks.

They continue until Pudding falls asleep.

Linlin sleeps beside her.

During the night Pudding wakes from a nightmare, reaches automatically toward Journal One, notices Mama beside her, smiles, lets go of the journal, and goes back to sleep.

For that moment, another person is remembering Pudding for her.

### Soul Feast boundary

Linlin can demand useful work from Pudding and still refuse to let that work erase her.

See [[production/characters/pudding/progression|Progression]].

## Katakuri

Katakuri reads people from the outside.

Pudding experiences people from the inside.

Their strengths contrast.

Katakuri may predict what Pudding intends to do while Linlin notices what she actually needs.

Katakuri's panic during the childhood hide-and-seek story also gives Journal One / family memory a mundane anchor rather than only solemn identity material.

## Sanji

Peak WCI should not require Pudding to exist only because she is a girl who can become Sanji's bride.

Any Sanji relationship must survive beyond "female romantic plot device."

Sanji genuinely finding her third eye attractive can still be a meaningful human beat if it emerges naturally.

## The people she carries

Pudding can inherit affection toward people she has never personally met.

That does not create a real reciprocal relationship.

She may know:

- how somebody's daughter smiled
- how somebody's mother smelled
- why somebody trusted a friend
- why somebody feared an object

She has to learn that emotional reality and autobiographical ownership are different things.
