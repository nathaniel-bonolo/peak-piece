# Charlotte Katakuri: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Katakuri is an elite Sweet Commander whose Observation Haki includes future sight and whose image of perfection/invulnerability is partly performance.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses him as a Luffy mirror with family protectiveness, reputation pressure, and technical mastery.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Literal future sight can dominate fight logic and become a high-tier requirement. His long life also supports a deeper blind spot than perfection pressure alone.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Katakuri predicts intent through extraordinary observation rather than literal future frames. His flaw is spending two centuries learning to anticipate behavior and gradually confusing prediction with understanding.

## Why this solution

This preserves terror and competence while giving him a human blind spot Pudding/family can expose.

## What we preserve

Mochi battlefield control, family protectiveness, disciplined image, observation expertise, commander role.

## What we intentionally reject

Literal future sight.

## Failure modes to watch

Prediction must fail for understandable reasons. Do not rename omniscience.
