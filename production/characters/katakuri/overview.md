# Charlotte Katakuri

## Core

Katakuri is a roughly 200-year-old commander.

He does not have literal future sight.

His "prediction" comes from centuries of observing:

- posture
- weight
- breathing
- eyes
- muscles
- weapons
- formations
- emotions
- habits

He is extraordinarily good at predicting what people are about to do.

He can still be wrong, fooled, overloaded, or fatigued.

## Flaw

Katakuri spent two centuries becoming excellent at anticipating what people will do and accidentally started treating that as understanding them.

He may correctly predict that somebody intends to leave and open the door for them when what they actually need is for somebody to ask them to stay.

His growth is not becoming socially incompetent.

It is learning that observation cannot answer every human question.
