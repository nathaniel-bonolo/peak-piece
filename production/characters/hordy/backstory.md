# Hordy Jones: Backstory

This file preserves the full V8 Devil's Hand progression with one major causal correction: **Hordy's childhood mine is not a Gem mine.**

## 1. The human world

Hordy is around ten to twelve when he secretly leaves home for Sabaody because he is fascinated by humans.

He meets human children. The friendship is genuine.

Adults notice the value of a young Fishman, exploit the friendship as access, and arrange a trafficking chain. The children do not fully understand the transaction.

Hordy is taken away believing the first humans he trusted sold him.

## 2. The mine

He is sold into a deep-sea **mineral extraction mine**.

The mine exists because Fishmen are useful underwater workers across many resources.

It teaches collective punishment: one rebellion can cost everybody food, sleep, safety, or skin.

## 3. Stories underground

Prisoners preserve stories about Fisher Tiger, a legendary Sea King figure, and an old Gem-injection technique.

The Tiger story degrades into a simple promise:

> **He had the technique. He became strong enough. He did the impossible.**

## 4. Hope kills people too

Small / low-grade Gems enter the mine through secondary finds, mixed cargo, theft, or nearby resource channels.

Raw crystals are not power sources.

The prisoners jury-rig mine electricity through crude converter equipment and charge them badly.

They know the legend's result, not the procedure.

They do not know safe charge, dose, transfer medium, physiological preparation, stabilization, or discharge rate.

Some users die. Some gain a terrifying burst and collapse. A rebellion fails. The mine punishes everybody afterward.

The correct conclusion is that the system forced desperate people into impossible choices.

Hordy learns:

> **People who rebel without enough strength or knowledge make everybody suffer.**

## 5. The first whip

A Fishman pushes an overseer. Guards reach for weapons.

Hordy sees the coming collective punishment, grabs a whip first, and beats the Fishman himself.

> **You fucking fool. You're going to make them hurt all of us.**

The guards notice that Hordy can control people with less force.

## 6. The offer

He accepts a kapo role with better food, a private room, movement, and limited authority because he believes:

> **I can hurt one Fishman less badly than humans will hurt fifty.**

## 7. It works

At first the rationalization gets evidence.

He reduces punishments, warns people, looks away, and lowers collective punishment.

That makes the trap durable.

## 8. He becomes useful

He learns workers' signals, predicts escape attempts, suppresses rebellions earlier, improves output, and becomes valuable to the institution.

## 9. Devil's Hand

A prisoner calls him **Devil's Hand**.

Hordy hits him once for performance and a second time because he is angry. He knows the second hit is not harm reduction.

## 10. The dead teenager

A young Fishman dies during a shift. Hordy orders the body moved coldly so the line does not stop.

That night the apology / burn / shower ritual begins.

## 11. The ritual hardens

Different victim. Same pattern: `To the...` → pen → paper → explanation → apology → fire → shower.

Early letters defend him. Later letters stop believing the defense.

Alcohol and smoking join the ritual.

## 12. He benefits

Do not sanitize this period.

Hordy keeps better food, enjoys being obeyed sometimes, drinks on privileges others lack, and occasionally hurts somebody because he is drunk or angry before inventing a strategic excuse afterward.

## 13. Transfer toward Fishman Island

Genauche hears about him through the wider underwater extraction network and arranges his transfer / release because he understands Fishman miners, coercion, unrest, and output.

The job gives Hordy freedom, authority, records, high-grade Gem infrastructure, and a route toward the real technique.

## 14. Tiger becomes an accusation

Hordy confirms Tiger really used a version of Gem injection and that Shirahoshi's father studied it.

His dead friends return to him psychologically.

He blames Tiger for giving people a story they could kill themselves chasing.

That blame is emotionally real and factually unfair.

## 15. Shirahoshi looks familiar

Her Tiger-inspired rebellion recreates the pattern Hordy learned to fear.

> **You idiot. You don't know what hope costs.**

He helps contain her while becoming increasingly certain she knows the real injection method.

## 16. Transactional alliance

Hordy has keys, access, followers, and knowledge of the extraction structure.

Shirahoshi has rebel legitimacy, followers, her father's research, and the technique.

He agrees to release her when the Tea Party reserve is high, Charlotte attention is stretched, and a large distraction appears.

## 17. Power without restraint

Hordy obtains the real method and becomes strong enough to impose the psychology the mine built in him:

> **I know what suffering is necessary. I have to carry the decision.**

## 18. "I will become the Sea King"

Nobody came for him underground, so he decides to become the savior himself.

## 19–24. Devil's Hand ending

The V8 ending remains locked in direction:

- Hordy's right hand chokes Shirahoshi
- an actual Sea King answers her and bites that hand off
- Hordy feels relief as well as pain
- he reaches home and automatically tries to continue the old ritual
- he writes the last apology left-handed
- the lighter slips and he leaves it
- the shower destroys the confession
- he dies with no deathbed absolution
- Shirahoshi arrives too late to read the letter
- Fishman Island remembers the monster; the audience knows the monster knew what he became

## Thesis

Canon Hordy asks what inherited hatred can become without personal originating injury.

Peak Hordy deliberately asks:

> **What happens when an oppressed person learns that becoming the oppressor's hand is the safest way to protect the oppressed?**
