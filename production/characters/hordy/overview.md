# Hordy Jones

## Core

> **If somebody is going to hurt us anyway, I should control how much it hurts.**

The belief begins as harm reduction and eventually turns him into another person everybody needs protection from.

## Childhood

Around ten to twelve, Hordy is fascinated by humans, sneaks to Sabaody, and forms genuine friendships with human children.

Adults exploit that friendship as access and arrange his trafficking. The children do not fully understand what happens.

## The mine

Hordy is sold into a **deep-sea mineral extraction mine**.

It is not primarily a Gem mine.

His enslavement belongs to the broader economy that exploits Fishmen for underwater labor.

The mine teaches collective punishment.

## How Gems enter his story

Gems are not the reason the mine exists.

They enter as a rumor about impossible liberation.

Prisoners hear that Fisher Tiger used an old Gem-injection technique.

Low-grade Gems sometimes appear as secondary finds, mixed-resource shipments, theft opportunities, or nearby extraction cargo.

The prisoners know raw Gems are not enough.

They jury-rig mine electricity through crude converters and try to reproduce a procedure they understand only through rumor.

They know the **result**. They do not know charge profile, dose, transfer method, physiological preparation, stabilization, or discharge rate.

Some die. Some become briefly powerful and collapse. Resistance fails. Collective punishment follows.

Hordy learns the wrong lesson:

> **Hope gets everybody hurt when you do not have enough power or knowledge to finish the job.**

## Devil's Hand

Hordy takes the whip first to prevent worse collective punishment, accepts a kapo role, and initially gets evidence that his logic works: thirty lashes become twelve, food theft gets ignored, inspections get warned about.

Then he becomes useful to the system, receives privileges, suppresses rebellion, improves production, drinks, sometimes enjoys authority, and sometimes hurts people simply because he can.

He is not a secret saint.

## Genauche

Years later Genauche recruits him through the wider extraction network because he understands Fishman miners and coercive labor.

The new position gives Hordy access to Fishman Island, records, high-grade Gem infrastructure, and a path toward the real injection technique.

## Tiger resentment

Underground retellings compress Tiger into:

> **Tiger had the technique. Tiger became strong enough. Tiger escaped the impossible.**

Hordy watched friends die imitating that result without the procedure.

He unfairly blames Tiger for giving desperate people hope they were not strong enough to survive.

## Shirahoshi

Shirahoshi's rebellion triggers Hordy's mine conditioning: hope → uprising → collective punishment.

Her father's research also makes her the person Hordy believes can provide the real injection method.

Their eventual arrangement is transactional.

## Ending

The existing Devil's Hand ending remains: Hordy gets the real technique, tries to become the Sea King nobody sent to save him, gets his right hand bitten off by the Sea King that answers Shirahoshi, returns home, writes the final left-handed apology, fails to burn it, lets the shower destroy it, and dies without public redemption.
