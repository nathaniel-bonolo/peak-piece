# Hordy Jones: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Hordy is deliberately revealed as someone whose hatred is not rooted in a personal atrocity committed against him. The Fish-Man District environment transmits hatred to him.

Peak Piece understands that function and intentionally rejects Hordy as the pure vehicle for that thesis.

## Peak thesis

> **What happens when an oppressed person learns that becoming the oppressor's hand is the safest way to protect the oppressed?**

The answer is not `trauma makes him right`.

A survival strategy can work just well enough to become an identity, and that identity can become oppressive long after the original excuse stops being true.

## Mine correction

Hordy's childhood mine is now a **non-Gem underwater mineral mine**.

This is deliberate anti-compression.

His enslavement should still exist if Gems are removed from the setting because Fishmen are exploited for underwater labor generally.

Gem injection enters through distorted Fisher Tiger folklore.

Prisoners obtain a few low-grade Gems, charge them badly with mine electricity, and imitate a result without knowing the real procedure.

That preserves the failed-hope chain without making Gems the cause of Hordy's entire childhood.

## Why direct suffering does not absolve him

Hordy repeatedly makes choices. Early choices are heavily constrained. Later choices are less constrained.

He reduces some punishments, stops rebellions, improves production, accepts privileges, keeps benefits, sometimes hurts people selfishly, knows when he is inventing excuses, and eventually seeks enough power to decide liberation on everyone else's behalf.

## Why the kapo role matters

The contradiction only works if collaboration produces real short-term benefits.

Twelve lashes really can be less than thirty.

The question is what happens when the person deciding between twelve and thirty starts believing that makes him morally entitled to hold the whip.

## Fisher Tiger

Hordy blaming Tiger remains intentionally unfair.

His friends died attempting crude Gem injection because the underground version of Tiger's legend convinced them impossible liberation might be reproducible.

Tiger is not the cause of their deaths. The mine and their desperate circumstances are.

## Shirahoshi / ending

The Shirahoshi transaction, real injection technique, Devil's Hand symbolism, Sea King injury, final private apology, and lack of public redemption remain intact.

## Failure modes

Do not make slavery automatically justify later violence, secretly reveal Hordy was a saint, make every punishment a hidden mercy, let the final letter erase responsibility, publicly rehabilitate him, or turn Tiger into the actual cause of his friends' deaths.
