# Hordy: Abilities

## Fishman physiology

Hordy is naturally physically powerful and adapted to underwater combat.

Years of mining, fighting, and labor enforcement make him physically dangerous before any injection.

## Practical intelligence

Hordy's most frightening pre-injection ability is not supernatural.

He becomes excellent at:

- reading workers
- predicting unrest
- noticing preparation
- identifying escape behavior
- suppressing rebellion before it starts
- understanding exactly how coercion changes group behavior

That intelligence makes him useful to both the mine and Genauche.

## Sea-creature handling

Like other Fishmen, Hordy has some capacity to work with sea creatures.

He becomes obsessed with the legendary possibility of becoming strong enough to deserve the title **Sea King**.

His mistake is increasingly treating that title as domination through force.

## Gem injection

Hordy eventually obtains the real [[production/systems/gem-injection|Gem Injection]] technique through his agreement with Shirahoshi.

It gives him an enormous temporary increase in output.

It does not repair his psychology, make him tactically omniscient, or remove the physiological cost.

The technique should feel like the power he spent his whole life believing would finally make rescue possible.

Instead, it gives Devil's Hand enough strength to impose himself.

## Final cost

The exact physiology remains open.

The final sequence requires:

- severe accumulated bodily damage
- right-hand loss to the Sea King
- blood loss
- enough remaining movement for Hordy to return home
- death shortly afterward

Do not lock fake medical detail before the system is ready.
