# Hordy: Relationships

## The human children

Hordy's first human friendships are genuine.

Adults exploit the relationship to capture him.

Hordy believes the children sold him out.

This should not become proof that friendship across species is fake.

It is a child's experience of trust being turned into property.

## The miners

Hordy is simultaneously:

- one of them
- their overseer
- their abuser
- sometimes the person reducing punishment
- the person who understands their rebellion well enough to prevent it
- somebody who benefits from the hierarchy

Some hate him.

Some fear him.

Some thank him.

All three reactions can hurt him differently.

## Charlotte Genauche

[[production/characters/genauche/overview|Genauche]] sees Hordy as evidence that her mine hierarchy can reward useful Fishmen.

She values his labor knowledge, predictive ability, force, cultural fluency, and efficiency.

Hordy sees her as both a better administrator than the humans who owned him and another person operating the cage.

He accepts her offer because access is useful to his own plan.

He remains beneath Charlotte authority no matter how competent he becomes.

## Fisher Tiger

[[production/characters/fisher-tiger/overview|Tiger]] is two incompatible symbols to Hordy.

He is:

- proof that a Fishman can directly free slaves
- the legend Hordy's friends imitated when crude Gem injection killed them

Hordy unfairly blames Tiger for the cost of hope.

This resentment becomes important when Shirahoshi adopts Tiger as a model.

## Shirahoshi

[[production/characters/shirahoshi/overview|Shirahoshi]] represents almost everything Hordy distrusts:

- public hope
- rebellion
- belief that people should stand up
- Fisher Tiger's legacy

Hordy's first instinct when she rebels is the same one the mine trained into him:

> **stop before everybody else gets punished**

He helps put her in a cell.

At the same time, Hordy believes her father discovered the real Gem-injection technique.

That makes Shirahoshi strategically essential to him.

Their later agreement is transactional.

### Their deepest disagreement

Hordy believes:

> **someone strong enough must decide what suffering is necessary**

Shirahoshi believes liberation cannot mean recreating ownership with a Fishman holding the whip.

### Final Sea King moment

Hordy tries to become the Sea King by force.

Shirahoshi calls one through the relationship/skill she built.

The Sea King comes for her.

Hordy is relieved.

## The person from the first whipping

Hordy never learns the prisoner's name.

That absence becomes the center of his final confession.
