# Hordy: Progression

## 1. Curiosity

Young Hordy is fascinated by humans and sneaks to Sabaody.

He makes genuine human friends.

Adults turn that access into trafficking.

## 2. Waiting

In the mine Hordy waits for Fisher Tiger, the Sea King, or anybody strong enough to save them.

Nobody comes.

## 3. Wrong lesson

He watches desperate prisoners rebel, attempt crude Gem injection, die, and trigger collective punishment.

He learns:

> **hope without enough power gets everybody hurt**

## 4. The whip

Hordy takes the whip from a human overseer and punishes a Fishman himself.

The humans offer him a kapo role.

His justification:

> **I can hurt one Fishman less badly than humans will hurt fifty.**

## 5. Evidence that the lie works

He really does reduce some punishments.

That gives the belief enough truth to survive.

## 6. Efficiency

Hordy learns to predict rebellion.

He stops unrest before it begins.

Production improves.

The mine rewards him.

His ability to understand Fishmen becomes a weapon against them.

## 7. Corruption

Not every later act is harm reduction.

Hordy keeps privileges, drinks, sometimes enjoys authority, hurts people while angry, and invents excuses after the fact.

He knows the difference.

## 8. Devil's Hand

The miners name him.

He hates the name because it is accurate.

The apology ritual becomes his only private admission.

See [[production/characters/hordy/letters|Letters]].

## 9. Genauche

Genauche brings him into the Fishman Island extraction system because his exact skill set is valuable.

Hordy accepts partly because the position finally gives him access to the system he wants to destroy.

## 10. Tiger resentment

He learns Tiger really used a version of Gem injection.

Instead of only seeing liberation, Hordy remembers the friends who died copying the legend.

He blames Tiger for giving desperate people dangerous hope.

## 11. Shirahoshi

Shirahoshi resembles the same pattern to him:

- Tiger admirer
- public hope
- rebellion
- people who may suffer if she fails

He helps contain/imprison her while also seeking her father's injection knowledge.

## 12. Transactional alliance

When the correct conditions arrive, Hordy releases Shirahoshi.

He gains the real injection technique.

## 13. Power

The technique finally gives him enough force to stop being beneath everyone else.

Unfortunately the person receiving that force is still Devil's Hand.

## 14. Sea King fantasy

Hordy decides:

> **If nobody came for us, I will become the Sea King myself.**

His childhood rescue fantasy becomes authoritarian self-appointment.

## 15. The hand

Hordy grabs Shirahoshi by the throat with his right hand.

A real Sea King comes for her.

It removes the hand.

Hordy experiences relief.

He no longer has to be the hand holding the whip.

## 16. Final letter

He returns home.

The ritual fails because the body required to perform it is gone.

He writes with his left hand.

He cannot burn the letter.

For once he lets the confession exist.

Then the shower destroys it anyway.

## 17. Death

He dies without public redemption.

Shirahoshi arrives too late to hear an explanation.

History remembers Devil's Hand.

The audience remembers the person who became him.

## Progression sentence

> **Hordy begins by controlling violence because he thinks controlled cruelty protects people from worse cruelty; he ends by becoming the person whose control everybody needs protection from.**
