# Sogeking

## Original function

Sogeking begins as psychological distance.

Usopp needs a mask to do something Usopp himself feels unable to do.

## Peak Piece development

After the Luffy duel and his growing preparation doctrine, the mask acquires a second meaning.

> **When Sogeking appears, Usopp came prepared.**

The mask does not give him a magic courage stat.

It becomes a ritual of readiness.

He has:

- studied the target
- prepared ammunition
- planned routes
- chosen fallback positions
- thought through likely failures

## Reputation

Over time, Sogeking can become frightening for a very different reason than Usopp intended.

Enemies begin associating the mask with operations where:

- communications fail
- officers get hit from impossible range
- escape routes close
- ammunition disappears
- decoys draw forces away
- the battlefield turns out to have been prepared in advance

The enemy's problem is not the mask.

The problem is what the mask implies has already happened.

## Endpoint

Usopp's final bravery cannot depend permanently on becoming Sogeking.

Eventually the story should place him in a situation where:

- there is no mask ritual
- no intelligence packet
- no prepared battlefield
- no fallback plan

and he acts anyway.

That completes the dream instead of replacing cowardice with a costume.
