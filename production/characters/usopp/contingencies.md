# Usopp: Contingencies

> **Foundational direction:** Usopp's greatest strength develops a psychological cost.

Preparation lets Usopp compete above his weight class.

That lesson eventually mutates into:

> **Even the strongest person protecting me can become the threat.**

An Evil Zoro / compromised-ally incident can crystallize that fear.

## Behavior

Usopp begins quietly thinking through ways to:

- escape Luffy
- immobilize Sanji
- disable Franky's weapons
- slow Brook
- avoid Robin
- survive Zoro

The plans are not perfect.

The important thing is that Usopp feels compelled to have them.

## Arc

His progression becomes:

> fear → preparation → competence → obsession → accepting that preparation cannot make life safe

The final brave-warrior payoff still requires a situation where:

- there is no adequate plan
- the information is bad
- the contingency failed
- the mask / ritual cannot save him

and Usopp acts anyway.

Preparation remains one of his defining strengths.

Paranoia prevents it from becoming consequence-free Batman competence.
