# Usopp: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Usopp is physically weaker than most major combatants but repeatedly wins through lying, gadgets, fear, observation, and matchup exploitation. His Water 7 duel uses prior knowledge of Luffy; Sogeking gives him psychological distance; Perona is beaten through deduction and deception.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon makes cowardice both comedy and character problem while repeatedly showing that Usopp becomes dangerous when he can think around stronger opponents.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

The brave-warrior dream can stall through repeated fear resets; post-timeskip upgrades risk becoming ammunition replacement rather than progression; Sogeking largely disappears despite doing important psychological work; Usopp lacks a sustained training environment built around asymmetric warfare.

## Peak Piece response

The Luffy duel becomes his explicit discovery that preparation lets him compete above his weight class. Sogeking means "I came prepared." A Revolutionary siege, Betty conditioning, Ghost Army deception, and a modular gun system develop that idea.

## Why this solution

This turns cowardice into raw material rather than deleting it. The same threat-generating brain produces panic without preparation and strategy with it.

## What we preserve

Lying, cowardice, humor, marksmanship, invention, Sogeking, psychological warfare, and the brave-warrior dream.

## What we intentionally reject

The assumption that courage means fear disappears and the childhood slingshot as a permanent serious endgame platform.

## Failure modes to watch

Preparation cannot become omniscience. Bad information, surprise, and wrong assumptions must hurt. His endgame must eventually require acting without prep.
