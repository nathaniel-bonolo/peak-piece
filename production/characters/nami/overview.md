# Nami

## Dream in daily life

Nami's world map is a continuing practice, not an endgame checkbox.

She constantly accumulates charts, weather observations, local maps, current information, lighthouse knowledge, route hazards, historical corrections from Robin, and current clues from Sanji.

## Greed / value

Nami can love money without becoming less economically discerning.

Her timeskip should make her **better** at price, value, regional markets, services, scarcity, and negotiation.

Greed is personality. Bad pricing judgment is not required to preserve it.
