# Nami: Timeskip

## Preserve Weatheria

The Weatheria core is useful and does not need replacement for replacement's sake.

The stronger change is what Nami **returns with**.

## Weather and perspective

She gains deeper practical understanding of weather systems, forecasting, climate anomalies, navigation, and weather technology.

Travel with / around Weatheria gives her unusual large-scale geographic perspective and rough coastlines, island positions, storm patterns, current hypotheses, and regional weather notes.

These are not a finished world map. Post-timeskip travel continually refines them.

## Markets and pricing

If Nami spends time pricing / selling weather services across different islands, she should become more discerning about local markets, demand, scarcity, service pricing, purchasing power, and negotiation.

Her greed can remain funny. Her economic judgment should improve.
