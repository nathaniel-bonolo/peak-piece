# Chopper: Abilities

## Human-Human Fruit

Chopper's forms are manipulated expressions of his Zoan Fruit.

## Rumble Ball

See [[production/systems/devil-fruit-pharmacology|Devil Fruit Pharmacology]].

The original Rumble Ball system is the product of Chopper experimenting on himself.

The compounds are not generic Devil Fruit steroids.

They are calibrated to specific Fruits.

## Medicine

Chopper remains the Straw Hats' doctor.

His medical competence should matter in:

- travel
- recovery
- disease
- poisoning
- long-term injury
- Devil Fruit pathology

## Research direction

Studying his own Fruit eventually leads Chopper to study other Fruits.

Luffy becomes one of the first major external subjects for Rumble-derived formulations.
