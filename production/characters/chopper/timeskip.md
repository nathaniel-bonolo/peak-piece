# Chopper: Timeskip

> **Foundational direction:** two years must causally produce the doctor / Zoan pharmacologist who later understands SMILE.

The exact destination is still open.

The result is not.

Chopper's two years should deepen him in areas such as:

- field medicine
- surgery
- pharmacology
- toxicology
- comparative biology
- Zoan physiology
- diagnosing unfamiliar species
- medical logistics
- distinguishing disease from Devil Fruit-linked pathology

The destination can preserve useful canon material if it actually produces these skills.

It should not reduce to:

> Chopper read medical books and came back stronger.

Nor should unrelated martial arts become the main explanation for his growth.

## Historical research lead

CerezaVeneris raised a useful research direction: some historical pirate crews lacked formal doctors and emergency medicine could fall to other ship specialists, including crude infection / amputation responses.

This remains a research lead until sourced properly.

A useful contrast may be that large state militaries such as the Marines possess more standardized medicine than ordinary pirate crews.

## Destination requirement

Whatever location eventually locks should give Chopper real problems to solve, real patients to treat, and real biological variation to understand.

The timeskip should feel like training through medicine rather than waiting in a library.
