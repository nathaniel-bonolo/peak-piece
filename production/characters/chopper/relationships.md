# Chopper: Relationships

## Luffy

Luffy is one of the people whose unconditional acceptance ends Chopper's need to make himself human enough.

Later, Luffy trusts Chopper enough to use experimental Fruit-specific Rumble formulations in desperate combat situations.

## Toko

Toko eventually becomes the emotional proof that Chopper's SMILE research mattered.

## Law

Law may become essential to solving nonlethal Devil Fruit extraction because of the surgical precision of the Ope-Ope Fruit.

The exact role is not locked.

## Killer and Kid

Killer gives the SMILE problem a continuing personal stake outside Wano.

Kid's desire to cure Killer can become a reason for another deeply uncomfortable Emperor alliance.
