# Smoker

## Core

Smoker remains a Marine who treats Luffy as a problem worth personally understanding rather than a bounty poster to forget after the arc ends.

## Sabaody command

Post-timeskip, Smoker is assigned to the strengthened Marine presence at Sabaody.

The role makes Sabaody his second Loguetown:

> a strategic gate where pirates can be stopped before the next sea makes them harder to contain.

His interest in Luffy is not only personal irritation.

Smoker has already seen how quickly Luffy grows, how often he survives, and how much chaos follows him.

He wants to stop that potential before it matures further.

## Return to Sabaody

Smoker's operation keeps the Sunny intact as bait, watches the likely return window, and responds when Brook's public activity makes the two-year reunion plausible.

The post-timeskip clash is valuable because Smoker remembers the version of Luffy who could not properly answer him.

The point does not need to be a ceremonial Smoker knockout.

It is enough that Smoker personally experiences how much the gap has changed while the Straw Hats still have to escape a prepared Marine operation.

## Tashigi

Tashigi remains part of the Sabaody command structure and can intersect with Zoro / Sanji / other Straw Hats during the collapsing interception operation.

Exact matchups remain open.
