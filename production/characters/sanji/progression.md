# Sanji: Progression

## Core flaw

Sanji expresses love through service.

That is one of his best traits.

It can also become self-erasure.

He is used to asking:

> What does everybody else need?

and much less comfortable asking:

> What do I need?

A dangerous version of this belief is:

> **My needs are cheaper than yours.**

If sacrificing himself keeps the crew functioning or protects somebody else, Sanji can treat that sacrifice as obviously rational.

## Why Whole Cake Island belongs to Sanji

Removing Germa cannot leave WCI with only:

> Charlotte intelligence selected the optimal hostage.

That explains **why Linlin takes Sanji**.

It does not explain **why this is Sanji's character arc**.

The emotional pressure point is usefulness.

Sanji knows the crew depends on him for:

- food
- planning
- morale
- combat
- logistics
- crisis judgment

He can twist that knowledge into:

> because I am useful, I should be the one who absorbs the cost

The Charlotte operation can exploit exactly that.

Sanji may cooperate with captivity longer than somebody like Luffy would because he believes his own suffering is cheaper than risking the crew.

## What the crew learns

Sanji's absence creates material problems.

That matters.

But the emotional answer cannot be:

> we need our appliance back.

The crew should make clear through action that Sanji's value is not conditional on what he provides.

This is a different shape of the usefulness problem from Robin.

Robin learned to treat usefulness as rent for existence.

Sanji can treat usefulness as a reason his own needs should always come last.

## Resolution direction

WCI should force Sanji to confront the difference between:

> serving people because I love them

and

> treating myself as expendable because I love them

He should not stop cooking, sacrificing, or caring.

He should learn that love does not automatically make his own life the cheapest resource in the room.
