# Sanji: Timeskip

## Character result

Sanji does **not** return having stopped liking women.

He returns having confronted a more basic failure:

> **Women are people, not objects that exist for his desire.**

He can still flirt, fall in love instantly, be dramatic, pursue women, get rejected, and be ridiculous about romance.

The change is respect.

## Return to Sabaody

A clean early proof is Sanji apologizing to Robin and Nami for how he treated them and promising to be more respectful.

Do not turn it into a therapy monologue. His behavior afterward has to prove it.

## All Blue

After reunion, Sanji's ordinary life includes fishing, species identification, unusual-catch inspection, marine ecology, and tracking evidence of currents connecting seas.

## Island mechanism still open

The strongest current mechanism is a flytrap / Lotus-Eater-style island whose mirage offers unlimited food, beautiful women, comfort, and effortless satisfaction.

Real jungle food may weaken the illusion.

This is an active proposal, not locked mechanics.
