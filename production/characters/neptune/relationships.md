# King Neptune: Relationships

## Shirahoshi

Shirahoshi is not his daughter.

She becomes one of the most visible opponents of the agreement he signed.

## Whitebeard

Whitebeard's system gave Neptune negotiating space because Fishmen could move and trade without becoming easy prey.

## Linlin

Linlin is not a friend.

She is the sovereign power Neptune accepts when the post-Whitebeard vacuum becomes intolerable.

## Jinbe

Jinbe understands why Neptune values durable arrangements even when those arrangements require ugly compromises.
