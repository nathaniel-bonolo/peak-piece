# Kaido: Abilities

## Dragon Fruit

Kaido's Fruit remains a major source of overwhelming force.

Peak Piece should keep the simple terror:

> **A dragon is a military problem before it is a boss fight.**

## Floating battlefield

Large-scale floating battlefield control requires continuous attention.

Hard combat forces Kaido to divide concentration between:

- fighting
- stability
- terrain
- the army standing on what he is moving

[[production/systems/gems|Gem]] support can extend the energy budget for unusually large feats, but it does not remove the concentration problem.

## Haki

Kaido uses Haki as one tool inside a larger combat doctrine.

Haki does not erase the strategic value of his Fruit, army, terrain, technology, or preparation.
