# Charlotte Genauche: Abilities

## Density-Density Fruit

> **Genauche can increase or decrease the density of material she touches.**

The power should remain one rule with second-order applications.

## Mining applications

Potential uses include:

- compacting unstable material
- reinforcing supports
- changing ore weight for transport
- increasing tool or drilling impact
- assisting material separation
- reducing transport load

Exact industrial methods should be audited when mine logistics are written.

## Combat

The same rule can support:

- increasing impact mass
- making objects difficult to move
- lightening material for rapid repositioning
- manipulating structural stability

She does not need unrelated bonus abilities.
