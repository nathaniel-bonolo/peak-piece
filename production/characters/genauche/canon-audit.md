# Charlotte Genauche: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Genauche is a Peak Piece original character with no direct canon counterpart.

## What canon appears to be doing

Her function is to embody respectable, optimized exploitation rather than cartoon sadism.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Fishman Island needs a Charlotte administrator who can genuinely improve mine safety while still defending coercive ownership.

## Peak Piece response

Genauche uses the Density-Density Fruit and professional administration to reduce collapses, improve rotations, and increase production while sincerely believing this makes the system civilized.

## Why this solution

The arc needs somebody who can improve conditions materially and still be wrong about treating people as contracted resources.

## What we preserve

Charlotte institutional competence and the unsettling possibility that exploitation can look orderly.

## What we intentionally reject

A one-note sadistic overseer.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not let better conditions equal freedom. Hordy never becomes her true equal.
