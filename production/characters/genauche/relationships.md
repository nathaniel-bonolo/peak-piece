# Charlotte Genauche: Relationships

## Linlin

Genauche is trusted with one of the empire's most strategically important extraction systems.

That implies real administrative competence.

## Hordy

She considers Hordy a valuable subordinate and may even believe his position disproves accusations of pure anti-Fishman domination.

To Hordy, the relationship proves the opposite: he can become indispensable without ever becoming equal.

## Shirahoshi

Shirahoshi rejects the legitimacy of the entire arrangement.

Genauche believes measurable improvements matter.

Their conflict should therefore be more interesting than "good rebel versus evil slaver."
