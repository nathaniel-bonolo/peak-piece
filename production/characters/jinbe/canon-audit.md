# Jinbe: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Jinbe is a Fish-Man Karate master, former Warlord, Sun Pirate, Whitebeard ally, and later Straw Hat.

## What canon appears to be doing

Canon uses him as an institutional bridge between Fishman politics, slavery history, piracy, Whitebeard, and Luffy.

Unless a source explicitly confirms authorial intent, this is **interpretation**, not a claim about Oda's private intention.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

Whitebeard's protection can remain abstract; Devil Fruit tradeoffs can get reduced to "Fishman should never eat one" instead of character-specific analysis.

## Peak Piece response

Peak Whitebeard makes trafficking and unsafe routes costly, giving Jinbe a concrete reason for deep loyalty. Jinbe personally rejects most Fruits because water is central to his style, while other Fishmen may rationally choose differently.

## Why this solution

His strength is institutional memory and judgment, not generic exposition-parent status.

## What we preserve

Fish-Man Karate, maturity, Whitebeard connection, Warlord history, politics, loyalty.

## What we intentionally reject

Species-wide simplistic Fruit rules.

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make Jinbe the answer machine. Let his institutional insight appear through choices.
