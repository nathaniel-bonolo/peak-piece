# Koala

## Current direction

Koala becomes **Revolutionary Army #2**.

This direction originated with **firefox6663355 / Fox**.

Nathan did not initially see why the rank itself was better than canon, but accepted the direction as worth developing because Koala's history and Revolutionary experience create room for a far larger role.

The rank now needs to be earned on the page rather than treated as a power-scaling sticker.

## Why Koala

Koala's movement from enslaved child to one of the people most responsible for organized liberation is the point.

Her importance should come from years of:

- field experience
- liberation work
- strategy
- organization
- Fishman Karate
- political trust
- surviving systems of slavery and then learning how to dismantle them

## Sabo

Sabo can be physically stronger in some contexts without automatically outranking Koala.

Koala can be the more experienced organizer / strategist while Sabo is more impulsive and combat-forward.

Exact command structure still needs scenes.

## Combat

Fishman Karate remains central.

Other weapon / Dragon-claw-adjacent ideas can be explored if they grow from training rather than turning her into a pile of cool moves.

## Standard

Do not make Koala #2 merely because the project wants "a strong woman."

Make her #2 because the organization, history, competence, and relationships actually support it.
