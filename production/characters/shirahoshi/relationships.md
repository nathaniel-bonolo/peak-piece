# Shirahoshi: Relationships

## Her father

Her father's research gives Shirahoshi knowledge that becomes politically and militarily dangerous.

## Fisher Tiger

Tiger is mentor, family influence, warrior ideal, and warning.

She admires what he did without romanticizing what Gem overuse did to him.

## Hordy

They are temporary allies against Linlin's extraction regime and ideological opponents over methods.

## King Neptune

Shirahoshi is not Neptune's daughter.

She opposes the deal he signed with Linlin while recognizing that he signed it under severe pressure.

## Straw Hats

The Straw Hats arrive as an accelerant, not as the creators of Fishman resistance.
