# Sabo

> **Foundational direction:** Sabo earns brotherhood onscreen rather than returning as Luffy's retroactive secret third childhood brother.

## Origin

Dragon rescues an orphaned Sabo during Revolutionary activity.

Sabo grows up inside the Revolutionary Army.

He jokingly treats Dragon as his father.

Dragon mostly refuses to indulge the bit.

## Luffy

When Sabo later learns that Monkey D. Luffy is Dragon's biological son, he immediately decides:

> **Oh shit. I have a brother now.**

Luffy does not agree.

Robin can tell Luffy about Dragon's adopted son before they meet.

Their first interactions should preserve the asymmetry:

Sabo thinks the family connection is obvious.

Luffy thinks this is some Revolutionary idiot making claims about him.

## Arc

Brotherhood is built through present-tense action.

The endpoint may eventually be genuine brotherhood, but it must be earned rather than unlocked through recovered childhood history.

## Why this version exists

It preserves Sabo's Revolutionary functions while avoiding the retroactive ASL insertion.

It also gives Dragon and Sabo a real parent-child texture and lets Luffy gain a brother **during the story** rather than discovering that he secretly always had one.
