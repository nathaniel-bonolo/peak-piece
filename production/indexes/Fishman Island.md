# Fishman Island

## Character spine

- Hordy — survival inside the system, then revolutionary power through the system's own tools
- Shirahoshi — anti-ownership resistance and inherited knowledge
- Genauche — exploitation made efficient and respectable
- Neptune — compromise under material pressure
- Fisher Tiger — liberation through direct action and self-expenditure
- Jinbe — durable security through institutions and alliances
- Whitebeard — autonomy and freedom of movement through deterrence
