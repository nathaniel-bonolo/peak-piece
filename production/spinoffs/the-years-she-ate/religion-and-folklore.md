# Religion and Folklore

## Principle

The Charlotte family does not need literal divinity.

If the same people remain alive while generations are born, age, tell stories, and die beneath them, ordinary history naturally becomes religious language.

Different islands should develop different traditions rather than one perfectly standardized church.

## Mama

Common associations can include:

- motherhood
- protection
- abundance
- judgment
- harvest
- lifespan
- family continuity

Some people fear her.

Some love her.

Some do both.

## Brulee

Common associations:

- mirrors
- thresholds
- watching
- safe passage
- household protection

An attuned mirror can be both useful infrastructure and sacred object.

## Pudding

Common associations:

- memory
- truth
- confession
- testimony
- history

A folk saying may insist nobody can lie to Pudding even though her actual Fruit and institutional reach are more limited than the myth.

## Katakuri

Common associations:

- foresight
- war
- protection
- safe roads
- inevitable judgment

Centuries of accurate anticipation can become stories that say Katakuri literally sees every future.

## Distortion rule

Folklore should exaggerate real history rather than function as a wiki page with incense.

A useful pattern:

> real event → repeated story → shortened moral → ritual → superstition
