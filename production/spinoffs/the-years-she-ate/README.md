# The Years She Ate

**The Years She Ate** is the companion historical anthology for the Eternal Matriarch era.

A conventional Big Mom flashback cannot carry four centuries of religion, war, family growth, political dependency, ritual, folklore, and institutional scars without turning the main story into a history lecture.

The anthology solves that by changing centuries and POVs.

Linlin is often **not** the focal character.

The reader experiences what she became through people who lived under, beside, against, or because of the Charlotte dynasty.

## Format

Each tale can stand on its own while contributing to the same historical organism.

Useful focal characters include:

- ordinary citizens
- offerings
- soldiers
- pirate victims
- Orchard targets
- merchants
- priests / local religious figures
- Charlotte family members
- Government officials
- enemies
- descendants meeting the same immortal Charlotte their ancestor once met

## Core artistic trick

Show an event directly in one era.

Then show a later generation remembering it incorrectly.

The reader gets to watch:

> **event → story → custom → religion → myth**

That makes four centuries feel lived rather than summarized.

## Main-story rule

Peak Piece must still work without reading the anthology.

The main story gets residue:

- a mirror nobody will break
- a Festival of Years
- a prayer to Katakuri
- a family proud of an ancestor's offering
- an ancient anti-Charlotte fort
- somebody casually saying Mama protected their grandmother's grandmother

The anthology explains why those details exist.
