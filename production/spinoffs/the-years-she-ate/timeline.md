# The Years She Ate: Timeline

Do not fill four centuries with dates merely because the space exists.

Lock eras only when a story, relationship, institution, or consequence needs them.

## Required historical anchors

- Linlin begins extending her own life / preserving selected family members
- early Charlotte territories discover the real bargain between protection and lifespan
- voluntary offering culture emerges somewhere organically
- Brulee mirror infrastructure becomes folklore / household ritual
- the Orchard develops from personal hunting into institution
- the Imu / Linlin strategic arrangement begins
- at least one major violation of that arrangement creates a long-lived burden
- Roger becomes a major Orchard obsession
- God Valley creates or deepens historical scars
- Roger penetrates Linlin's Poneglyph security
- Roger's illness becomes known to Linlin
- Roger dies and starts the Great Pirate Era
- the Orchard expands dramatically in the new era
- current-era Straw Hats begin triggering Roger-pattern recognition
- Linlin's eventual defeat creates a succession / legitimacy crisis

## Rule

Do not make every century equally busy.

History contains long periods of stability, forgotten decades, local crises, and events that mattered only to one island.
