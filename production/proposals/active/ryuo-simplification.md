# Proposal: Ryuo Simplification

## Question

Does Ryuo perform a unique story/system job, or is it simply a named advanced Armament technique?

Peak Piece is open to cutting or simplifying it.

## Constraint

Do not preserve a separate Haki layer merely because canon gave it a special name.

Do not cut a meaningful cultural or mechanical distinction before identifying what would be lost.
