# Proposal: Burgess Gets the Mera-Mera no Mi

Author: **firefox6663355 / Fox**

## Direction

Instead of Sabo inheriting Ace's Fruit, Jesus Burgess obtains the Mera-Mera no Mi.

Nathan currently prefers this direction, but the exact Dressrosa scenario is not finished enough to lock.

## Why explore it

- avoids turning Sabo into an Ace replacement through the same power
- gives the Blackbeard Pirates a symbol that is personally intolerable to Luffy
- makes a future Burgess conflict emotionally specific rather than only factional
- lets Ace's legacy remain contested rather than neatly returned to Luffy's side

## Open work

- exactly how Burgess wins / steals / receives the Fruit
- what Luffy can reasonably do in Dressrosa
- what this changes about Sabo's entrance and combat identity
- when the emotional payoff happens
