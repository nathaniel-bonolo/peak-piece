# Proposal: Chopper Timeskip Audit

This is a **research lead**, not a locked historical claim until sourced properly.

Possible contrast: Marines as a large state military may have better standardized healthcare than ordinary pirate crews.

Drum Kingdom may also be useful if it contributes real institutional / medical knowledge rather than appearing only because it is already the medicine kingdom.
