# Proposal: Fable Loki and the Fake Ragnir

## What this replaces

An earlier direction used **Ragnir** as a Ratatoskr + Mjolnir concept:

> Loki's hammer ate a squirrel Zoan.

This proposal deletes that literal creature-weapon.

It also avoids simply giving Loki another giant Mythical Zoan dragon package.

## Fable-Fable Fruit

### Unawakened

A lie Loki tells can become true when other people **genuinely believe** it.

The Fruit does not reward somebody politely pretending to believe him.

Belief has to be real enough to change the world.

### Awakened

Loki's own genuine belief can alter reality.

Hard rule:

> **Loki cannot choose what he believes. He can only make reality follow something he genuinely accepts as true.**

If he knows he is pretending, nothing happens.

## The fake hammer

Loki's hammer is not the real divine Ragnir / Mjolnir.

It begins as an ordinary or fake hammer.

Loki convinces people that it **is** the legendary weapon.

Their belief gives the lie enough reality that the fake hammer becomes capable of doing what the myth says the real one should do.

The delicious part is that the weapon can become functionally real **because the fake convinced the world it was real**.

This makes the hammer an expression of Loki's core power rather than a second unrelated gimmick.

## Blindness direction

Loki's removed eyes can connect to belief mechanics.

Direct sensory contradiction is a powerful source of doubt.

Removing sight can become a deliberate sacrifice that reduces the amount of reality constantly telling him:

> **that thing you believe is false**

The Odin parallel is useful, but the story should make the choice belong to Loki rather than merely checking a mythology box.

## Weakness

Information can hurt Loki.

If an opponent can make Loki genuinely believe that he is:

- bleeding
- trapped
- losing
- unable to perform something

his own power can make that belief dangerous.

This creates a natural Usopp information/deception battle without requiring Usopp to overpower Loki physically.

## Overlap risk

The concept needs enough constraints to avoid feeling like generic reality warping or a copy of other belief/comedy powers.

The "cannot choose belief" rule is the current core constraint.

Exact scope, duration, number of simultaneous beliefs, contradiction behavior, and awakening limits remain open.

## Nidhoggr

[[production/proposals/active/nidhoggr-creature|Nidhoggr]] should remain a separate creature/ecology direction rather than being forced into Loki's Fruit.

A useful Elbaf split is:

- **Loki = lies / belief**
- **mythic animal messengers = information**
- **Nidhoggr = physical mythology / ecology**
