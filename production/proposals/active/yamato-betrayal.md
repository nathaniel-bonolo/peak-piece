# Proposal: Yamato as an Insider Betrayal

## Seed

CerezaVeneris raised the Honnō-ji structure while discussing [[production/references/history/oda-nobunaga|Oda Nobunaga]].

The useful causal shape is:

> trusted insider understands the fortress, timing, command structure, and vulnerabilities -> waits until Kaido is committed -> turns his preparation against him

## Why it may fit

[[production/characters/kaido/overview|Kaido's]] biggest Soul Feast advantage is that Wano is his prepared battlefield.

Yamato could matter by compromising that preparation from inside rather than merely switching teams and punching Kaido.

The exact:

- motive
