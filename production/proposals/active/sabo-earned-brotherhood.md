# Proposal: Sabo Earns Brotherhood Onscreen

This proposal keeps Sabo alive but removes the retroactive secret third-brother / noble-childhood insertion.

It remains contested.

## Direction

Dragon rescues an orphaned Sabo during Revolutionary activity.

Sabo grows up inside the Revolutionary Army and jokingly treats Dragon as his father.

Dragon mostly refuses to indulge the bit.

When Sabo later learns that Luffy is Dragon's biological son, his conclusion is immediate and ridiculous:

> **Oh shit. I have a brother now.**

Luffy does not agree.

Robin can tell Luffy about Dragon's adopted son during / after the timeskip.

When they finally meet, Sabo claims brotherhood and Luffy responds more like:

> **You're that idiot Robin told me about. You're not my brother.**

The relationship then has to become real through shared action.

## Why explore it

- preserves Sabo as a Revolutionary character with useful plot functions
- removes retroactive ASL childhood insertion
- makes brotherhood something built in the present
- gives Dragon/Sabo an actual parent-child texture

## Conflict

Some collaborators prefer Sabo to remain dead / preserve canon ASL history.

Do not treat this proposal as locked until the room resolves that direction.
