# Proposal: Brook as an Ancient Linlin / Orchard Victim

This is **not part of foundational Brook**.

It only becomes chronologically possible if [[production/proposals/active/ancient-linlin-soul-dynasty|Ancient Linlin]] survives.

## Direction

Roughly fifty years before the main story, Brook's own pirate crew becomes valuable enough to attract Charlotte/Orchard attention.

The causal chain is institutional rather than coincidence:

> significant pirate crew → Charlotte intelligence evaluates them → Orchard system marks them → high-value harvest operation

Pudding / Charlotte harvest specialists prepare the crew.

The crew is eventually turned into soul weapons.

## Brook's Yomi-Yomi complication

Before Brook's resurrection has occurred, the Yomi-Yomi no Mi has a claim on his soul's postmortem return.

Proposed narrow rule:

> **Before the destined resurrection, Brook's soul cannot be permanently severed into a finished Charlotte weapon.**

This is not generic immunity after resurrection.

Repeated harvest failure can make Brook unusually knowledgeable about souls before Linlin finally kills him and his Fruit returns him later.

## Potential payoff

If accepted, this can explain:

- why mention of Linlin can break Brook's normal comedic rhythm
- why his soul knowledge is unusually practical
- why Soul Feast interactions with loose soul material matter personally
- why his dead-promises arc has one wound he cannot reduce to a logbook

## Failure conditions

Reject or rewrite this extension if it:

- makes Brook's foundational dead-promises arc dependent on Linlin
- makes the world feel smaller rather than causally connected
- requires Ancient Linlin to survive only to save Brook's backstory
- turns every important old pirate into a Charlotte victim

Brook remains Peak Brook if this entire proposal is deleted.
