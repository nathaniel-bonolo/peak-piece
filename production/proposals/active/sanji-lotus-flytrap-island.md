# Proposal: Sanji Lotus / Flytrap Island

Sanji's timeskip island behaves like a predatory paradise.

A plant / island ecosystem creates a mirage built around what traps him most easily: unlimited food, beautiful women, comfort, and no reason to leave.

Possible phrase:

> **Prince of a jungle that tried to eat him.**

Eating **real** food from the jungle may weaken the mirage.

The stronger lesson is not `Sanji learns not to be horny`.

It is discernment and respect: desire is not reality, and a beautiful woman is not an object created to satisfy him.

A Lotus-Eater parallel is a useful research direction. A Japanese parallel also needs proper research before canonization.

Do not paste mythology into the story merely because the correspondence is neat.
