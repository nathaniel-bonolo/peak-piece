# Proposal: May Weller

Author: **CerezaVeneris**

This is a collaborator OC and is not Peak canon yet.

## OC discipline

Original characters should be used sparingly.

Cameos and filling genuine causal gaps are fine. Do not create giant plot-central OCs merely because the rewrite wants new toys.

May should earn screen time through her own causal path.

## Existing core

May is an East Blue character descended from a disowned Charlotte with Baroque Works / Whitebeard-family / Charlotte-family connections under review.

Her Zhenniao Zoan body turns substances she consumes into toxins or medicines. The Fruit rewards study, dosage, compatible blends, delivery, persistence, antidotes, timing, and interaction knowledge.

Soap is decontamination, not a universal antidote.

## Pudding / WCI placement under review

An older pitch partly used May / Guard Duck as a Reiju replacement who could warn Sanji about Pudding.

That role needs re-evaluation because Peak Pudding may not betray Linlin in the same canon way.

Any warning should come from May's actual knowledge and relationship, not from a requirement to reproduce the Reiju beat.

## Possible later aerial role

May could potentially become a Charlotte-side aerial fighter later.

The idea is attractive because she can fly and it could demonstrate restrained OC integration.

But capability is not causality.

Before placing her, answer why May is there, which state/personality is present, what she believes she owes the family, and whether the arc needs **May specifically** rather than "a flying unit."

If the only answer is tactical utility, do not add her.

## Connection discipline

Avoid making May secretly central to every major faction.

Her existing chain can work if she remains low in institutional importance. Do not keep adding hidden connections.
