# Proposal: May Weller

Author: **CerezaVeneris**

This is a collaborator OC and is not Peak canon yet.

## Current concept

May Weller is an East Blue character descended from a disowned Charlotte.

Major affiliations in the current pitch:

- Baroque Works
- Whitebeard family
- Charlotte family connection

She has a long-standing crush on Miss All Sunday / Nico Robin.

She later undergoes memory alteration involving [[production/characters/pudding/overview|Pudding]].

## Zhenniao Fruit

The bird concept is load-bearing.

Current one-sentence direction:

> **May is a Zhenniao Zoan whose body turns substances she consumes into toxins or medicines.**

The Fruit should not become an excuse for unrelated Mythical Zoan powers.

## Current biological/combat package

Accepted in principle:

- merganser/waterfowl-descended body concept
- terrestrial speed and snake-hunting adaptation
- poison derived from what she consumes
- oily carrier
- up to five toxin types in a loadout
- fire vulnerability because feathers/oil are dangerous around heat
- oil contamination can be rapidly stripped from skin with soap before enough toxin is absorbed
- toxin blends require study
- compounds can interfere, amplify, or alter one another
- evasive fighting because direct impact is dangerous
- attacking her in close range creates a poison-contact headgame

### Important soap rule

Soap is decontamination, not a universal antidote.

Once toxin has meaningfully entered the body, washing the skin does not magically cure poisoning.

## Bird-bone biomechanics

CerezaVeneris supplied an academic source:

[[production/references/science/avian-bone-biomechanics|Avian Bone Biomechanics]]

The paper supports avian bones as lightweight structures adapted for bending and torsional resistance.

It does **not** directly prove a blanket claim that bird bones are especially weak to blunt impact.

Peak Piece therefore keeps May's direct-impact vulnerability as an accepted **combat-design choice** without pretending the paper proves more than it does.

## Combat identity

May should feel like a dangerous evasive glass cannon rather than a generic high-durability Mythical Zoan.

A clean high-level blunt hit can radically swing the fight.

Her opponent still has to decide whether making physical contact is worth the poisoning risk.

## Study requirement

This Fruit should reward knowledge.

May becomes stronger through:

- dosage
- compatible blends
- delivery method
- persistence
- antidotes
- timing
- interactions
- understanding which compounds cancel one another

The Fruit's ceiling is partly intellectual.

## Haki compatibility issue

Peak Haki is a learned discipline using [[production/systems/gems|Gems]].

Different Zoan forms should not arbitrarily gain or lose Haki because the Fruit says so.

If May and Cordial have unequal access to Observation or Armament, the stronger explanation is psychological/training access:

- different dissociative states may access different learned skills
- memory reconnection could later allow some skill crossover

This remains under review.

## Dissociation

CerezaVeneris's original concept gives the Zoan a strong independent consciousness and memory separation.

Peak review currently prefers a narrower version:

- one autobiographical memory system
- trauma-created compartmentalization
- Zoan instinct may influence dissociative states
- May names a survival state "the bird"
- different states may access memories through different associative routes

This is not resolved.

## Pudding connection

This character could become a formative failure for Pudding.

Instead of a simple "separate magical hard drive," Pudding may discover that memories are networked.

Cutting one association does not necessarily remove every route into the same experience.

This could help establish why memory editing risks changing identity.

## Charlotte Cordial

The current pitch includes a state/personality called Charlotte Cordial produced after memory alteration.

Potentially strong direction:

Cordial should not simply be "evil assassin May."

She may genuinely experience the Charlotte family as home while another state experiences the same history as captivity.

Neither state has to be fake.

## Connection discipline

Avoid making May secretly central to every major faction.

The current:

Baroque Works -> Whitebeards -> Charlotte ancestry

chain can work if she remains low in institutional importance.

Do not keep adding major hidden connections.
