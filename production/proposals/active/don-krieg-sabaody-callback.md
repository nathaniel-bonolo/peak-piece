# Proposal: Don Krieg Ship Callback

## Seed

During Return to Sabaody, the ship Zoro destroys as part of his post-timeskip showcase could belong to Don Krieg.

The visual callback is intentional:

- Mihawk once destroyed Krieg's ship around Baratie
- two years later Zoro can casually reproduce a version of that image
- the moment communicates growth without a speech about how much closer Zoro is to Mihawk

## Why it is only a proposal

The callback does not earn itself merely by being recognizable.

Before promotion, the story needs a causal reason for:

- why Krieg is at Sabaody
- what his crew looks like after the East Blue defeat
- why this ship is in the path of the reunion / escape
- whether Krieg himself needs to appear or whether the callback becomes distracting

Do not contort Return to Sabaody just to manufacture fan service.

If the causal setup becomes clean, the image can be excellent.
