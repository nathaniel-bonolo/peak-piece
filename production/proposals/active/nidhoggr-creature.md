# Proposal: Nidhoggr as an Actual Creature

Author: **CerezaVeneris**

## Direction

Nidhoggr exists as a physical mythological creature / Elbaf ecological horror rather than being reduced to "Loki has another dragon Zoan."

Current visual direction:

- European mole-like skull / head language
- subterranean, predatory body plan
- modified wing-arms functioning as digging limbs rather than standard flight wings
- enormous even by giant standards, but not lazy island-scale escalation
- roots / underground relationship to Yggdrasil
- visual influence from burrowing monster designs rather than another Kaido silhouette

## Mythology

Nidhoggr's association with roots, corpses, darkness, Hel / Niflheim, and gnawing beneath the world tree can inform ecology and abilities.

Cold / ice remains an attractive possibility, not a locked checklist item.

## Separation from Loki

This proposal pairs naturally with [[production/proposals/active/loki-fable-fake-ragnir|Fable Loki]].

Keeping Nidhoggr physically separate lets Loki's power remain about belief while the mythological creature can have its own ecology, scale, and story function.
