# Haki: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline
Canon Haki has Observation, Armament, and Conqueror's branches with advanced techniques including future sight and coating.

## Problems
Literal future sight can dominate fight logic; Haki can become the universal explanation for victory; late escalation can crowd out Devil Fruits, technology, terrain, specialization, and information.

## Peak response
Haki remains a martial tool, not the fight operating system. There is no literal future sight. Different cultures preserve different applications. High-output use interacts with Gems while skill/efficiency remain decisive. Luffy learns from Amazon Lily specialists rather than one master teaching three bars.
