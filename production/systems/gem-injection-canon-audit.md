# Gem Injection: Design Audit

## Canon baseline
Canon Hordy's crew uses Energy Steroids that produce power at severe aging cost.

## Peak response
Gem injection becomes an ancient/degraded Fishman technique that bypasses safer energy limits at devastating physiological cost. Tiger uses it during liberation; Hordy reads him as proof it works; Shirahoshi reads the same history as proof of cost.

## Failure mode
Exact physiology remains open. Do not invent fake medical detail for aesthetics.
