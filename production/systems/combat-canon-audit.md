# Combat: Canon Audit

One Piece fights already mix powers, absurdity, matchup, environment, and will. Peak Piece pushes against long-run escalation collapsing into stronger punch / stronger Haki / new transformation.

Information, preparation, terrain, objectives, logistics, morale, attention, resources, and matchup can decide fights too. Usopp + Betty winning through deception and surrender pressure is a model example.

## Failure mode
Do not replace Punch Piece with Chess Piece. Characters still need to behave like themselves.
