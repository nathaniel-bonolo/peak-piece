# Technology: Canon Audit

One Piece contains cyborgs, Pacifistas, Den Den Mushi, weather science, seastone, lineage-factor research, and wildly uneven technology. Peak Piece's concern is not realism for its own sake; it is **adaptation**. Major inventions should create maintenance, supply, countermeasure, training, black-market, and political consequences.

## Failure mode
Do not explain engineering just because the repo knows it. Technology should create story problems and solutions.
