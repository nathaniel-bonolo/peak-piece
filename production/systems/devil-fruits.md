# Devil Fruits

## Core rule

A Devil Fruit should be understandable from a clear central premise.

Applications should mostly be consequences of that premise, not unrelated bonus powers.

Good test:

> **Can the Fruit be summarized in one sentence?**

Examples:

- Luffy is rubber.
- Kuzan can turn into and control ice.
- Linlin can extract souls and place soul material into other things.
- Genauche can change the density of material she touches.

## Gems do not power the Fruit

Devil Fruits function without [[production/systems/gems|Gems]].

Normal use is limited by the user's body, stamina, concentration, heat, and the Fruit's mechanics.

Gems can provide external energy for unusually large, sustained, or repeated output.

They do not create abilities the Fruit did not already have.

## Design rule

Prefer:

> new situation -> new application

over:

> new situation -> surprise new power

## Awakening

An awakening should still emerge from the Fruit's core property.

For [[production/characters/luffy/abilities|Luffy]], the Fruit remains rubber-based.

The Nika / hidden Zoan retcon is removed.

## Fishmen and Devil Fruits

A Fishman taking a Devil Fruit is a **character-specific tradeoff**, not automatically a bad decision.

Losing swimming is especially expensive for a Fishman, but the value depends on role and fighting style.

- [[production/characters/jinbe/overview|Jinbe]] depends heavily on water and Fish-Man Karate, so most Fruits would cost him far more than they give.
- Jack can rationally accept a powerful Zoan if the command/combat value is worth sacrificing free swimming.

Peak Piece should ask what the specific person gains and loses instead of applying a species-wide rule.

## Pharmacology

[[production/characters/chopper/overview|Chopper]] discovers that Devil Fruit expression can be temporarily altered using Fruit-specific compounds.

See [[production/systems/devil-fruit-pharmacology|Devil Fruit Pharmacology]].

This is not a universal potion that gives every Fruit user free new abilities.

## Zoans

Zoans may have instinctive or will-like properties, but Mythical Zoans should not become an excuse for unrelated ability bundles.

If a Mythical Zoan appears, its abilities still need a coherent core.

The current [[production/proposals/active/may-weller|May Weller]] proposal is being judged under this rule.
