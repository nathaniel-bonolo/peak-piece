# Gems: Design Audit

There is no direct canon equivalent.

Gems solve several Peak Piece problems at once:

- extreme output can have downstream cost without being made visually unimpressive
- Devil Fruits and Haki can operate at normal scale without external fuel
- top-tier factions can amplify already absurd users
- strategic energy becomes trade, war, mining, status, and technology
- the ancient flood can create material geopolitical consequences
- Fishman access to submerged deposits becomes strategically important
- Whitebeard's protection can disrupt a global extraction system

## What Gems are not

Gems are **not** permission slips for powers.

Devil Fruits and Haki work without them.

Gems are external energy amplifiers.

They increase scale, duration, repetition, or output without granting:

- new abilities
- skill
- knowledge
- control
- immunity to non-energy limits

## Aura

A hostile reading can imagine every Kuzan-scale feat being followed by an accounting lecture.

That is not the intended story experience.

Gems themselves can be visible as jewelry / bling.

The exact reserve accounting is writer-facing.

> **The world sees the jewels. The audience sees the miracle. The writers see the ledger.**

Kuzan freezing the ocean should still read as:

> **holy fuck, Kuzan**

The production bible can separately remember that the feat was expensive enough not to become an infinitely repeatable default answer.

## Three ceilings

Do not assume every user optimizes toward the mechanic's theoretical maximum.

Separate:

- **ability ceiling** - what the mechanic permits
- **character ceiling** - what this user would discover / choose
- **faction ceiling** - what surrounding specialists and infrastructure can help unlock

Apoo can possess the raw potential for strategic resonance without personally inventing Death Note.

Queen can raise the faction ceiling.

> **Optimization belongs to characters who optimize.**

## Racism

> **Gems feed Fishman racism. They do not cause it.**

Prejudice has independent social and historical causes.

Gem economics give institutions material incentives to preserve conditions that make Fishmen cheap to exploit.

The economic layer is reinforcement, not a total origin story for racism.

## Failure modes

- do not turn the story into mana accounting
- do not make every major feat require dialogue about reserves
- do not let Gems erase skill or personality
- do not explain all racism as economics
- do not push every Fruit user toward optimal military use
- do not keep adding Gem math once the downstream story question is answered

> **When the spreadsheet has done enough to make the adventure consistent, close it.**
