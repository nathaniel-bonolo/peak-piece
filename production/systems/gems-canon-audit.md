# Gems: Design Audit

There is no direct canon equivalent.

## Current purpose

Gems provide a narrow strategic tool: extreme energy density, portable burst reserves, optional amplification of abilities that already exist, and a military market for exceptional storage crystals.

They are **not** an energy source, the electrical grid, oil, or a universal technology substrate.

## Causal-compression correction

Earlier design let Gems spread too easily across unrelated domains.

That created a real architectural risk: combat, ancient history, racism, Fishman geopolitics, technology, and imperial power could begin sharing one master variable.

The correction is:

> **Gems amplify. They should rarely originate.**

Fishman exploitation exists without Gems. Anti-Fishman racism exists without Gems. Whitebeard's protection matters without Gems. The World Government has material power without Gems. The flood changes many resources, not only Gems. Normal technology works without Gems. Hordy's enslavement does not begin in a Gem mine.

## Failure modes

- do not turn Gems into oil
- do not turn Gems into the electrical grid
- do not make every advanced machine secretly Gem-powered
- do not make every major conflict ultimately about Gems
- do not make Gem scarcity explain ordinary Haki rarity
- do not let reserves erase throughput, skill, personality, or setup
- do not explain Fishman oppression through one mineral
- do not add a Gem answer merely because one is available
- do not expose writer-facing reserve math as dialogue

> **When the spreadsheet has done enough to make the adventure consistent, close it.**
