# Technology

Status: Draft

## General rule

Technology must have logistics.

Ask:

- What powers it?
- What does it cost?
- Who maintains it?
- How long does it take to prepare?
- What specialist staff does it require?
- What happens when it overheats or fails?
- Why is this better than a conventional alternative?

## Pacifistas

Pacifistas are powerful weapon platforms, not cheap replacement police.

They can annihilate ordinary pirate crews, but they are expensive and preparation-heavy.

Expected constraints include:

- major Gem consumption
- heat buildup
- charging
- calibration
- coolant
- software
- transport
- specialist maintenance
- limited flexibility

They are best in planned wars and prepared deployments.

### Heat as counterplay

Full output is terrifying.

Sustained output raises temperature.

Eventually the unit must throttle, cool, or expose vulnerable vents.

This makes their strongest performance create its own tactical weakness.
