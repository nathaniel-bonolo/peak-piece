# Combat

Status: Draft

## Core rule

Strength alone does not determine fights.

Combat outcomes can depend on:

- position
- information
- logistics
- terrain
- objectives
- preparation
- timing
- attention
- reserves
- morale
- environment
- matchups
- opportunity cost
- ability to convert an advantage

## Competence

A competent character can lose without becoming stupid.

Prefer:

> You made the correct decision. The opponent prepared for that too.

over:

> You forgot how your own power works so the plot could continue.

## Information

Fight choreography should often follow:

action -> information -> adaptation -> counter

Every exchange should ideally change what at least one side knows.

## Power escalation

Avoid endless "bigger attack" progression.

A new move is most satisfying when it is a new use of an existing rule.
