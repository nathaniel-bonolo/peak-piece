# Devil Fruits: Canon Audit

## Canon strength
One Piece's strongest power-writing idea is a weird central property pushed into creative applications.

## Problems
Mythical Zoans can become unrelated ability bundles; the Nika reveal changes Luffy's long-established premise; large-scale powers can ignore cognition, stamina, infrastructure, or world adaptation.

## Peak response
Prefer one-sentence cores. New situation, not new power. Gems extend energy budgets but do not turn Fruits on. Mythical status is not permission for arbitrary bonuses. Luffy remains rubber.
