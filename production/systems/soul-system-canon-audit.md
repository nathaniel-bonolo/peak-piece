# Soul System: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Linlin extracts lifespan/soul, taxes Totto Land residents, creates Homies, and uses fear in soul interactions.

## Peak opportunity

The immediate question is:

> **Why fear?**

Peak Piece treats fear as an extraction route rather than the metaphysical definition of soul value.

That creates a useful distinction:

- **yield** follows the strength of the emotion making extraction possible
- **quality** follows the strength/coherence of the person the material comes from

This supports Linlin, Pudding, Cracker, Brook, Final Service, and the Hyena interaction without requiring arbitrary exceptions.

## Why this is not meant to become soul mana

The system is written precisely backstage so emotional events can remain consistent.

The story should not quantify souls onscreen.

If the audience remembers "soul quality tier" instead of the person giving up years of their life, the scene failed.

## Failure modes

- do not make every emotion a clean stat
- do not show numerical soul values
- do not make willingness equal moral endorsement
- do not let Linlin perfectly predict every unfamiliar soul interaction
- keep each unit of soul material tied to somebody's life
