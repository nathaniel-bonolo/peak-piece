# One Piece: Recharted Discord

Peak Piece and **One Piece: Recharted** grew from the same early collaborative rewrite project before diverging into two creative directions.

Recharted focuses primarily on repairing / reworking post-timeskip *One Piece* while keeping pre-timeskip much closer to canon.

Peak Piece is the broader reimagining housed in this repository.

Neither is presented as the "correct" version of the other.
