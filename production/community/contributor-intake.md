# Contributor Intake

Before assigning work, make sure the newcomer has seen [[production/FOUNDATIONS|Foundations]]. Peak Piece is broader than the original post-timeskip repair premise, and nobody should discover that only after contributing substantial work.

Do not invent a formal title the moment somebody joins the writers' room.

If a new collaborator already has rewrite notes, videos, chapter work, research, or critiques, read the work first.

Let their repeated contribution pattern reveal the useful role:

- story development
- critique / review
- research
- character work
- systems
- adaptation
- something else

A role should describe what somebody actually contributes, not become ceremonial onboarding.

Final continuity / merge authority remains separate from ideation. A contributor can generate excellent ideas without needing repository authority, and authority does not mean one person must originate every idea.
