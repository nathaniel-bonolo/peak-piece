# Straw Hat Dreams as Daily Life

## Core rule

> **A Straw Hat's dream should affect how they live on ordinary days.**

Dreams should shape what they notice, where they go on a new island, what books they buy, who they talk to, and what knowledge accumulates.

Do not turn every island into a quest checklist. The point is lifestyle.

## Sanji — All Blue through food and currents

Sanji fishes, identifies species, inspects unusual catches, checks stomach contents, asks where market fish came from, and compares what he sees with books.

If a fish associated with another sea appears somewhere strange, Sanji has a reason to suspect currents and bring the clue to Nami.

## Nami — map as a living project

Nami continuously integrates coastlines, local charts, currents, weather, lighthouses, hazards, Sanji's biological current clues, and Robin's historical geography.

## Robin — history on the map

Robin contributes old names, historical borders, submerged ruins, migration, lost ports, and records explaining why modern geography looks strange.

## Chopper — medicine and ecology

Chopper naturally seeks local medical texts, remedies, pharmacies, disease records, and unusual biological information.

## Ordinary island routine

When circumstances allow, the Straw Hats can peel away by interest: Nami to navigators / charts, Sanji to fish markets / docks, Chopper to clinics / pharmacies, Robin to libraries / archives.

Sometimes several of them end up in the same library for completely different reasons.

## Rule

Do not write `character remembers dream because plot needs dream`.

Prefer `character has been practicing dream all along, so relevant knowledge already exists when plot collides with it`.
