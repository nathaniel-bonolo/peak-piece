# Small Moments

Large emotional payoffs need ordinary life underneath them.

These notes collect faction moments that may only take:

- a panel
- a page
- a short scene
- a recurring visual gag

They are not only "wholesome scenes."

A small moment can be:

- comedy
- affection
- routine
- friction
- ritual
- embarrassment
- irritation
- character texture
- family texture
- continuity
- accidental setup

## Rule: it does not need future importance

A small moment may exist purely because it is funny, affectionate, awkward, mundane, irritating, or strange.

Do not force every gag to become foreshadowing.

But continuity remembers.

A joke that was genuinely just a joke may later become emotionally meaningful because the characters remember living through it.

That later meaning should feel discovered, not retroactively stapled on.

## Organic comedy

Prefer:

> **character + circumstance + existing rules**

over:

> character temporarily loses their brain because the gag needs it

[[production/lore/factions/small-moments/charlotte-family/backwards-salute-batch|The backwards-saluting Biscuit Reserve batch]] is the model:

Pudding rushes a routine process, creates one harmless defect, and Cracker decides fixing it costs more than living with it.

Nobody has to be incompetent.

The result is still stupid.

## Metadata

Use tone/function metadata rather than creating separate `funny/`, `emotional/`, `domestic/`, or `setup/` folder trees.

One moment can serve multiple functions.
