# Robin Gets Dragged In

Robin usually watches the crew's stupid games instead of joining. That makes the rare time they drag her into one matter.

She begins annoyed. Then she realizes she is actually having fun.

Do not turn it into a friendship speech. A tiny smile, an unexpectedly competitive move, or Robin quietly not leaving when the game continues is enough.

This is [[production/characters/robin/identity|Robin discovering what she likes when usefulness is not the question]].
