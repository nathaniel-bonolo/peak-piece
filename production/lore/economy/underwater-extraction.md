# Underwater Extraction

## Core economic fact

Fishmen are extraordinarily valuable to the post-flood world because they are naturally suited to **underwater extraction and infrastructure**.

That advantage exists whether Gems exist or not.

Useful work includes mineral mining, rare-metal extraction, old-world salvage, shipwreck recovery, deep-sea construction, submerged-infrastructure maintenance, trench exploration, and resource surveying.

Gems are one unusually strategic resource inside a much larger economy.

> **Remove Gems and underwater extraction is still valuable.**

## Fishman Island

Fishman Island sits around a major submerged mineral basin containing multiple valuable resources.

Gems happen to be one of the most militarily valuable because of their energy-density niche.

Do not reduce Fishman Island to "the Gem country."

Its strategic value comes from population, location, underwater expertise, trade access, labor, routes, infrastructure, and the broader basin.

## Global labor market

Fishmen matter outside Fishman Island too.

A deep-sea platinum mine in North Blue may depend heavily on Fishman workers even if the best platinum deposit is nowhere near Fishman Island.

If Fishmen are poor, restricted, trafficked, or politically weak, employers can obtain labor cheaply.

If Fishman Island becomes safe, wealthy, and able to employ its own people, distant workers gain an alternative.

Then the North Blue mine owner may have to raise wages, improve conditions, negotiate honestly, or invest in expensive human-compatible underwater technology.

Freedom on Fishman Island therefore changes Fishman bargaining power **across the world**.

## Why the World Government order benefits from weakness

Do not simplify this into one secret memo ordering Fishman poverty.

Many actors can have aligned incentives:

- mine owners
- slave traders
- shipping networks
- contractors
- buyers of cheap minerals
- governments with submerged deposits
- corrupt officials
- nobles
- smugglers

The World Government supplies law, force, trade control, and selective enforcement inside that ecosystem.

Some beneficiaries can publicly condemn slavery while refusing to ask why their supply is so cheap.

## Why nobody simply captures Fishman Island and industrializes it

Direct occupation is a nasty investment problem.

An outsider must spend money on mines, specialist machinery, ports, transport, housing, security, supply lines, processing, and skilled staff.

Then it must hold a population physically better adapted to the environment.

A revolt can expel the occupier and seize the equipment.

Now the invader has lost its capital **and created an independent competitor with leverage**.

For profit-maximizing actors, trafficking, middlemen, fragmented dependence, and coercive contracts can be safer than sovereign occupation.

## Whitebeard's innovation

Whitebeard is not necessarily the first person physically capable of taking Fishman Island.

He is the first major power in the current history with the combination of capability and incentive to **underwrite Fishman autonomy instead of extracting ownership from it**.

He supplies deterrence while leaving the productive system in Fishman hands.

That lets Fishman-owned mines, merchants, routes, and capital accumulate.

He makes exploitation more expensive by making "no" more realistic.

## Why powerful interests want Whitebeard gone

Whitebeard can raise costs across slavery, trafficking, underwater mining, shipping, labor recruitment, predatory contracts, and resource procurement.

Gem prices may rise too, especially for military buyers, but the Whitebeard effect survives if Gems are deleted from the setting.

## Why Linlin did not take over earlier

Before Whitebeard, a direct Charlotte takeover faces the same greenfield risk as anyone else.

Whitebeard changes the board by proving a protected Fishman-controlled economy can function and by helping create / secure mines, equipment, routes, experienced labor, trade relationships, and merchant confidence.

After he dies, Linlin finds functioning infrastructure plus a security vacuum.

Whitebeard:

> **I provide security so you can bargain for yourselves.**

Linlin:

> **I provide security, therefore I get the controlling share.**
