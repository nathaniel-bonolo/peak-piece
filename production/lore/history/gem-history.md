# Gem History

Natural Gem deposits once existed across a much larger accessible world.

The Gems themselves are storage crystals, not energy sources.

Roughly 800 years ago, the sea level rose dramatically.

Many Gem deposits became submerged alongside ordinary mines, cities, farms, factories, infrastructure, and other resource systems.

Gem loss is one consequence of a much broader resource shock.

See [[production/lore/history/flood-resource-order|Flood Resource Order]].

## Pre-flood stockpiles

Imu / the emerging World Government may have accumulated valuable raw Gems, processing capability, converter knowledge, and many non-Gem resources.

Flooding can make existing high-grade Gem **capacity** disproportionately valuable.

It does not mean the Government stored eight hundred years of charge.

## Haki

Basic Haki does not require Gems.

Gem access can help elite factions sustain especially high-output applications.

Gem scarcity does **not** explain why ordinary Haki knowledge declined.
