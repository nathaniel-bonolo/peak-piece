# Fishman Liberation History

## Extraction era

Fishmen are strategically valuable because they can access and work the submerged world's resources: minerals, rare ores, salvage, infrastructure, Gems, and more.

Existing prejudice is amplified and exploited by governments, pirates, traffickers, and extraction interests.

Gems are one profitable pressure inside a broader extraction economy.

## Fisher Tiger

Fisher Tiger becomes a liberation figure through direct action.

A Gem researcher in his circle, Shirahoshi's father, helps recover an old Fishman technique for routing **charged Gem energy** directly through the body.

Tiger uses and overuses it.

The technique is something Tiger used. It is not why Tiger matters.

## Whitebeard

Whitebeard makes movement safer, frees slaves, returns kidnapped Fishmen, protects Fishman-owned trade / extraction, and lets workers bargain from a stronger position.

That effect reaches beyond Fishman Island and threatens business models built on cheap coerced underwater labor.

## Why this model is unusual

Direct occupation is expensive and risky because a failed occupier can lose its underwater infrastructure to the population it tried to dominate.

Whitebeard avoids the colonial investment trap because he does not need to own the mines.

## Linlin

After Whitebeard dies, Linlin restores security while converting protection into extraction control, quotas, a dominant share, and hostage leverage.

She inherits a functioning productive system and changes who owns the leverage.
