# Flood Resource Order

## Core correction

The ancient flood is **not a Gem event**.

It submerges and destroys entire systems of material access.

The emerging World Government / Imu can prepare by stockpiling and securing **many categories of resources, infrastructure, and knowledge** before rivals lose them.

Gems are one category inside that larger preparation.

## What can be lost or buried

Examples include mines, rare ores, industrial materials, farmland, factories, roads, ports, archives, specialist machinery, old technologies, processing centers, trade routes, and Gem deposits.

## Prepared scarcity

A faction that prepares before catastrophe can emerge with leverage because it controls **scarcity itself**, not one magic commodity.

Different societies may become dependent on different resources.

One needs an industrial metal. Another needs old machinery. Another needs seed infrastructure. Another needs salvage access. Another needs high-grade Gem capacity. Another barely cares about Gems.

That heterogeneity is important.

## Gem boundary

High-grade raw Gems preserve storage **capacity**, not ancient charge.

Deeply charged Gems leak, stress, and require maintenance.

Imu can possess an eight-hundred-year-old crystal stockpile; he cannot possess eight hundred years of untouched electricity.

## Fishmen after the flood

Fishmen become more strategically important because far more useful material geography is underwater.

Their value is not limited to Gem mining.

## Anti-compression rule

Do not use the flood to explain everything.

Do not use Gems to explain the flood.
