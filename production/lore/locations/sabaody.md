# Sabaody Archipelago

## Post-timeskip function

Peak Sabaody becomes the New World's **Loguetown-style chokepoint**.

Pirates still use it as the gateway toward Fishman Island and the New World, but after the Straw Hats vanish the Marines increase surveillance, patrols, intelligence coverage, and rapid-response capacity.

The archipelago remains a civilian / criminal / pirate space rather than becoming one giant Marine fortress.

## Smoker's command

[[production/characters/smoker/overview|Smoker]] is placed in charge of the Marine operation because the assignment fits both his instincts and history.

He wants to stop dangerous pirates before the New World turns them into larger problems.

Luffy is the obvious personal case.

## The Sunny as bait

The Sunny remains after the crew disappears.

Instead of Kuma guarding it for two years, the Marines eventually seize / impound the ship and preserve it as bait.

Destroying the vessel would remove one of the best reasons for the missing crew to return.

The Marines can inspect and inventory it while confiscating portable valuables / contraband.

That creates a practical reason nobody simply steals the ship and a comedy reason for Nami to experience immediate spiritual death on return.

## Return window

2D3Y gives Marine intelligence a timing clue even if they do not perfectly decode every layer of the message.

Brook's highly public concert almost exactly two years later gives Smoker enough confirmation to prepare a serious interception operation.
