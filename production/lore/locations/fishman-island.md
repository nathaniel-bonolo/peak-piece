# Fishman Island

## Strategic position

Fishman Island sits in one of the world's major submerged **mineral basins**.

The basin contains multiple valuable resources. Gems are one strategically important mineral among them.

Fishmen can work deep underwater environments that are difficult, dangerous, or lethal for ordinary human miners.

That makes Fishman labor and expertise valuable throughout the flooded world, not only on Fishman Island.

## Why nobody simply conquers it

Fishman Island is difficult to occupy directly because of environment, logistics, troop transport, local Fishman strength, hostile terrain, and long supply lines.

There is also an investment trap.

An outside power that builds expensive mines, ports, equipment, and transport infrastructure may lose all of it if Fishmen revolt and expel the occupier.

The revolt can then inherit the technology and become an independent competitor.

Outside powers therefore often prefer kidnapping, trafficking, forced labor, middlemen, unequal contracts, trade restrictions, and political pressure.

## Whitebeard era

Whitebeard claims the people rather than treating the island as a colonial possession.

His deterrence opens routes, reduces trafficking, protects Fishman-owned extraction, and improves bargaining power.

This affects workers elsewhere too: a Fishman in an abusive North Blue mine has more leverage when going home is a realistic alternative.

## Post-Whitebeard vacuum

Whitebeard's death closes routes and reopens trafficking pressure, but the mines, equipment, labor knowledge, and trade relationships built during the safer era still exist.

## Linlin agreement

Linlin does not have to build Fishman Island's extraction economy from zero.

She steps into the security vacuum and changes the protector's role.

In exchange for protection she takes control of major strategic extraction, including Gems, through output obligations, a dominant Charlotte share, labor quotas, and Fishman "residents" who function as hostages.

Whitebeard:

> **Security so you can bargain for yourselves.**

Linlin:

> **Security, therefore I get the controlling share.**

## Sea-creature taming

Fishmen have a natural spectrum of ability to tame, communicate with, or work alongside sea creatures.

The legendary extreme is someone capable of taming an actual Sea King.

This is not a royal bloodline power.
