# Death and Fakeout Direction

## Principle

If a scene is written and emotionally staged as a death, do not casually erase the consequence afterward.

This is not a mandate to kill characters for grit.

It is a mandate to stop using fake death as free emotion.

## Locked / strong directions

- **Pell dies.** The sacrifice keeps the consequence it was written to have.
- **Pound dies.** Do not convert the emotional beat into another survival reveal.

## Open

- **Bon Clay:** unresolved. Do not lock survival/death merely to preserve a later clone mechanic.
- **Kin'emon:** current preference is that he returns to Wano with purpose and eventually dies for real, but exact timing remains story work.

## Standard

A death should change relationships, information, leadership, grief, logistics, or future decisions.

Do not preserve a character solely because the setting has historically preserved people after apparent death.
