# Return to Sabaody: Canon Audit

## Canon function

Canon Return to Sabaody reunites the crew, shows their new designs / abilities, uses the Fake Straw Hats for comedy, and demonstrates that Pacifistas no longer represent the same threat.

## Peak Piece direction

Peak Piece keeps the reunion and comedy but gives the Marine presence a stronger causal spine.

Sabaody becomes the New World's **Loguetown-style chokepoint**.

Smoker commands the Marine presence because he wants to stop Luffy before Luffy reaches the potential Smoker already recognizes.

The Sunny becomes deliberate bait rather than requiring Kuma to stand guard for two years.

The 2D3Y timing plus Brook's public concert makes a prepared return operation plausible.

Chopper becomes the opening focal character, recognizes that the Fake Straw Hats are fake, and is caught when the Marine sweep hits the imposters. Recovering him gives the crew a concrete objective that turns the showcase into a fighting retreat.

The arc now also pays off Luffy's post-Marineford growth: he clearly outperforms Smoker but does not waste time finishing a fight that no longer advances the mission.

## What we preserve

- reunion excitement
- Fake Straw Hat comedy in compressed form
- Pacifista benchmark
- visible post-timeskip growth
- Sabaody as the gateway toward Fishman Island / New World

## What we intentionally change

- Fake Straw Hats no longer exist only because the gag needs them
- Chopper is not fooled for an implausibly long time
- the Marines are prepared for the return
- Kuma does not spend two years functioning as ship security
- Chopper's capture gives the crew a concrete rescue objective
- the showcase grows out of an interception operation rather than a random sequence of opponents
- Luffy does not need to render Smoker unconscious simply to certify a powerscaling win
- Tashigi's Zoro conflict is driven by weapons and custody rather than proving herself as a woman swordsman

## Failure modes to watch

Do not make the Marine ambush so perfect that the Straw Hats only survive because the Marines become stupid again.

Do not make Luffy leaving Smoker feel like fear or an unresolved 50/50 fight. The fight must clearly show that Luffy is stronger before he chooses the mission over a needless finish.

Do not let the retreat become so tactical that the reunion loses its joy.
