# Soul Feast / Wano War: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Wano culminates in the Onigashima raid: the alliance attacks Kaido's stronghold, many simultaneous matchups resolve across the island, Big Mom becomes a second Emperor threat, Kaido lifts Onigashima, Queen deploys Ice Oni, and Luffy reaches Gear 5.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

- too many story lanes resolve as private boss fights
- the alliance attacks a prepared Emperor with a comparatively fragile plan
- Big Mom/Kaido competence can bend to keep the raid alive
- Onigashima floating functions heavily as a timer
- Ice Oni is a temporary Chopper problem with larger strategic implications
- SMILE army logic and Beast Pirate doctrine are underdeveloped
- Gear 5 brings the Nika/Zoan retcon and new tonal/mechanical problems

## Peak response

The Straw Hats and Law raid Kaido's weapons factory first, capture Queen, seize defensive intelligence, learn important parts of his doctrine including Sky Coffin, false-flag Totto Land, and engineer a collision between two hostile military powers before committing their own force.

They do **not** know everything. Kaido still owns the home field and has unknowns.

## Luffy and casualties

A hostile summary can make this sound like Luffy casually tricks thousands of innocents into dying. That is not the design.

The collision is between **Kaido's and Linlin's military forces**. Soldiers will die. The alternative is also war: directly attacking Kaido at full strength with a weaker force.

Peak Piece should keep civilians away from the central collision as much as the plan can reasonably manage and should not treat war as bloodless chess.

## Queen intelligence and Momonosuke

Queen's captured files are why the alliance knows Sky Coffin exists. Momonosuke is prepared in advance as a counter rather than suddenly becoming emergency utility after the trap starts.

## Cracker

At army scale, canon-like biscuit soldiers create a command problem. If Cracker consciously puppets every unit, his real superpower is impossible parallel cognition. Peak's soul substrate + limited Pudding memory template provides distributed autonomy.

The **repo** may describe that architecture. The manga only needs to establish the soldiers can act independently under standardized orders.

## Final Service

This is not heroic martyrdom. A soldier who expects to die tonight if the army loses can rationally spend twenty of forty future years to improve the chance of surviving with the remaining twenty.

The horror is that lifespan has become battlefield capital.

## Matriarch

The Matriarch stays. It is not a planned RPG-item quest. Linlin spends twenty of her own years, Brook interferes with the released soul, Zoro destroys the intended vessel, and an ordinary Wano katana becomes the available vessel. The name comes later.

## Backstage systems

The repo contains mirror constraints, Gem budgets, command structure, resonance logic, Cracker autonomy, terrain traps, and supply assumptions.

The manga does not present that as a PowerPoint.

> **The spreadsheet is ours. The audience gets the adventure.**

## Counter-build criticism

A hostile reading can make Soul Feast sound like:

> Unit A exists -> invent Unit B to counter it -> invent Unit C to counter Unit B.

That is not the intended development method.

Several interactions emerged by following consequences that were already independently useful.

Example chain:

1. Apoo's amplified sound can destroy hearing.
2. Kaido's own troops therefore use hearing protection because they know their weapon.
3. Linlin's side experiences the problem and Perospero has an obvious material response: quick candy molds.
4. The same sound also damages mirrors and terrain, so protecting ears does not solve the battlefield.
5. Hyena traits were selected because Kaido finds low fear / pain tolerance useful.
6. Only later does that established trait collide with Linlin's fear extraction.
7. The terrain-fracture behavior makes Queen's old uneconomic Sky Coffin groundwork viable.

The rule is:

> **Follow the consequence, not the optimization.**

If a counter exists only because the author wanted a balanced matchup, audit it harder.

If it is the obvious thing this person would try after seeing the problem, refusing to use it can become the plot hole.

## Authority versus ideation

Do not summarize the alliance as "Luffy invents a false flag."

Law, Robin, and other planners can create the operation.

Luffy authorizes participation.

Do not summarize Sky Coffin as "Kaido engineers a terrain trap."

Queen creates the project.

Kaido decides whether it is worth the cost.

## Aggregate-tone criticism

"This is different from canon" is not a defense if the finished chapters are less fun.

The real test begins when pages exist.

The design repo is allowed to look like engineering documentation.
