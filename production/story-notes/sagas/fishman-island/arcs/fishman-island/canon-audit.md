# Fishman Island: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Fish-Man Island centers on inherited racism, Otohime and Fisher Tiger's legacies, Hordy's inherited hatred, Shirahoshi as princess/Poseidon, Vander Decken, and Big Mom's protection/tribute relationship.

## Canon function

The arc studies prejudice reproduced across generations. Hordy's lack of personal originating grievance is deliberate to that function. Shirahoshi contrasts softness with enormous inherited power.

## Peak response

Fishman Island sits in a major submerged mineral basin.

Fishmen are strategically valuable because of underwater extraction **generally**, not because one magic resource explains their economy.

Gems are one high-value military commodity inside that broader system.

Whitebeard protects people, routes, Fishman-owned extraction, and bargaining power.

Linlin fills the post-Whitebeard security vacuum with coercive protection / extraction.

Neptune accepts it desperately. Shirahoshi organizes against it. Hordy is a former child miner / kapo from a **non-Gem mineral mine**. Genauche represents efficient exploitation.

## Anti-Gemflation correction

Earlier architecture let too many Fishman Island causes terminate at Gems.

The current rule is:

> **Gems amplify incentives and structures. They should not create them.**

Remove Gems and:

- underwater Fishman labor is still valuable
- exploitation still exists
- racism still exists
- Whitebeard's protection still raises bargaining power
- direct occupation is still risky
- Linlin still sees value in inheriting a functioning extraction / trade system

Adding Gems makes some military and economic stakes more intense.

It does not create the whole causal machine.

## Intentional Hordy replacement

Peak Piece understands canon Hordy's inherited-hatred thesis and deliberately gives him another:

> **What happens when an oppressed person learns to survive by becoming useful to the machinery oppressing him?**

Hordy's new mine is deliberately non-Gem. Gem injection enters later through liberation folklore, which reduces causal compression without removing the Tiger / Hoshi / failed-injection chain.

## Shirahoshi warning

Removing Poseidon does not justify replacing her with Generic Badass Woman. She remains gentle, wants to become a warrior because she admires Tiger freeing people, and eventually learns liberation requires more than fighting.
