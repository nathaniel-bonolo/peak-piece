# Fishman Island: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline

Canon Fish-Man Island centers on inherited racism, Otohime and Fisher Tiger's legacies, Hordy's inherited hatred, Shirahoshi as princess/Poseidon, Vander Decken, and Big Mom's protection/tribute relationship.

## Canon function

The arc studies prejudice reproduced across generations. Hordy's lack of personal originating grievance is deliberate to that function. Shirahoshi contrasts softness with enormous inherited power.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies

- Hordy is more thesis than contradictory person.
- Shirahoshi's central importance is hereditary chosen power.
- Vander Decken consumes plot without helping the rewrite's political question.
- Big Mom's island relationship is strategically thin when reduced mainly to candy tribute.
- Whitebeard's protection needs concrete daily meaning.
- Fishman liberation should exist before Luffy arrives.

## Peak response

Fishman Island becomes the largest strategic Gem basin. Whitebeard protects people and routes. Linlin fills the post-Whitebeard security vacuum with coercive protection/extraction. Neptune accepts it desperately. Shirahoshi organizes against it. Hordy is a former child miner/kapo. Genauche represents efficient exploitation. The Straw Hats arrive during an existing political struggle and accelerate it.

## Intentional thematic replacement

Peak Piece understands canon Hordy's inherited-hatred thesis and deliberately gives him another:

> **What happens when an oppressed person learns to survive by becoming useful to the machinery oppressing him?**

If inherited hatred remains useful, it can live elsewhere in Fishman society rather than remaining Hordy's entire function.

## Shirahoshi warning

Removing Poseidon does not justify replacing her with Generic Badass Woman. She remains gentle, wants to become a warrior because she admires Tiger freeing people, and eventually learns liberation requires more than fighting.

## Full Hordy comparison

The earlier audit only summarized Peak Hordy as "former child miner / kapo."

The developed version is more specific.

Peak Hordy:

- begins fascinated by humans
- is trafficked after adults exploit a genuine childhood friendship
- learns collective punishment in a Gem mine
- watches friends die chasing crude injection because they believe liberation stories
- takes the whip because he thinks controlled cruelty reduces worse cruelty
- receives real evidence that this works in some cases
- becomes increasingly efficient at suppressing rebellion
- improves the mine
- accepts privileges
- becomes addicted to alcohol
- writes private apologies
- sometimes hurts people for selfish reasons
- resents Tiger because he associates hope with dead friends
- sees Shirahoshi as another dangerous source of hope
- gains the real injection technique
- tries to become the Sea King nobody sent to save him
- loses Devil's Hand to the Sea King that answers Shirahoshi
- dies privately apologizing without public rehabilitation

The rewrite is not claiming:

> canon Hordy failed because Oda forgot to give him trauma

It is saying:

> we understand canon's inherited-hatred function and prefer a different Hordy whose survival strategy corrodes into collaboration, control, and failed liberation

The final standard is not whether he resembles canon Hordy.

It is whether the Peak version produces a better story.
