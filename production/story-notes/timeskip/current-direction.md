# Straw Hat Timeskip: Current Direction

## Luffy

Amazon Lily / Rusukaina remains the training base.

Kuja specialists teach Haki subareas; Boa personally handles Conqueror's.

Sabaody and Marineford are the first major cracks in Luffy's worldview. The two-year timeskip improves his tools; it does **not** complete his emotional or philosophical growth.

## Robin

Kuma sends Robin directly to the Revolutionary Army base.

Her development comes through safety, archives, intelligence work, adult peers, choice, and learning that usefulness is not rent for existence.

Do not require formal therapy scenes if the lived environment already does the job.

## Usopp

Revolutionary field environment remains the direction: reconnaissance, sabotage, marksmanship, traps, preparation, asymmetric warfare, and Betty's resolve amplification.

## Sanji

Current direction: more respectful relationship to women without deleting attraction / romance, stronger lived All Blue practice, and the Lotus / flytrap island as an active mechanism proposal.

## Nami

Weatheria core remains. Expand the result: weather expertise, rough world mapping, market/pricing skill, and a map that keeps accumulating crew knowledge.

## Chopper

Location can survive; result needs deeper causality into field medicine, Zoan pharmacology, and later SMILE research.

## Zoro

Mostly preserve canon training. Add more Mihawk / Perona life.

## Franky

Timeskip core is broadly fine. The larger Franky issue is later Egghead material.

## Crew dreams

Dreams should shape ordinary island behavior after reunion rather than disappearing until the ending.
