# Avian Bone Biomechanics

## Source

Federica Buccino, Paolo Bruzzaniti, Sara Candidori, Serena Graziosi, Laura Maria Vergani.

**Tailored Torsion and Bending-Resistant Avian-Inspired Structures.**

*Advanced Engineering Materials* 24 (2022), 2200568.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/adem.202200568

Source supplied by **CerezaVeneris**.

## What the paper supports

The paper treats avian bone as a lightweight biological structure with internal ridges and struts that help resist bending, torsion, and buckling-related loads.

That is useful inspiration for the Zhenniao body design in [[proposals/active/may-weller|May Weller]].

## What it does not prove

This paper does **not** directly establish a universal rule that:

> bird bones are especially weak to blunt impact

Peak Piece should not cite it as if it did.

May's direct-impact vulnerability can still be a fictional combat-design decision consistent with a lightweight evasive bird body, but the exact blunt-damage claim should not be presented as a conclusion of this paper.
