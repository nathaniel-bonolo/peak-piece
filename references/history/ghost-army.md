# Ghost Army

The U.S. Army's 23rd Headquarters Special Troops, commonly called the **Ghost Army**, was a World War II tactical-deception formation. It combined visual deception such as inflatable vehicles with fake radio traffic, staged sound, false headquarters activity, and impersonation to make enemy forces misread Allied strength and location.

## Peak Piece use

This is a structural reference for [[characters/usopp/timeskip|Usopp's Revolutionary siege]]. Usopp does not need eight thousand real reinforcements if he can manufacture enough **evidence** of eight thousand reinforcements that the enemy makes decisions as if they exist.

The useful lesson is not "inflatable tanks are cool." It is:

> **The enemy fights the situation it believes exists.**

That pairs naturally with [[characters/belo-betty/abilities|Betty]], who can amplify the resolve to act on a conclusion the target already believes.
