# Honne and Tatemae

Reference raised by **CerezaVeneris** while discussing SMILE.

Background:
https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honne_and_tatemae

## Interpretation

One useful reading of canon SMILE is that forced smiling evokes tension between inner feeling and socially visible expression.

Peak Piece treats this as **interpretation**, not a confirmed statement of author intent.

## Rewrite value

The forced emotional expression is worth preserving even while the military mechanics of SMILE are rebuilt.

That lets Peak Piece keep thematic imagery without requiring Kaido to knowingly destroy most of his army for a tiny chance at useful Zoans.
