# Whole Cake Island: Canon Audit

## Canon baseline
Canon WCI centers on Sanji's Vinsmoke family, arranged marriage to Pudding, Big Mom's Tea Party, the Charlotte family, the Soul-Soul Fruit, Totto Land, and Luffy retrieving Sanji.

## What canon appears to be doing
The arc contrasts biological and chosen family while presenting a fairy-tale utopia funded by coercion.

## Problems Peak Piece identifies
- Sanji's value becomes overdependent on Germa ancestry/marriage machinery.
- Linlin's hunger instability can override the Emperor when the plot needs survival.
- Pudding's memory/identity implications deserve more long-term development.
- Consequences should flow directly into later political pressure rather than partially reset.

## Peak response
Sanji is targeted because he is operationally important, isolatable, and leverageable. General Linlin remains competent and bizarre. Pudding's memory work gains identity cost. Damage to WCI and Fishman Gem access directly helps cause Soul Feast.

## Failure modes
Do not make WCI an economics lecture. Do not make Linlin emotionally normal. Do not turn Sanji into only a logistics asset.

## Sanji-specific replacement

The hostile criticism that "better hostage logic does not automatically make a Sanji arc" is valid.

Peak's answer is not to restore Germa automatically.

It is to give WCI a Sanji-specific internal problem:

> **Sanji treats his own needs as cheaper than everybody else's.**

Charlotte intelligence can exploit that because a person who thinks self-sacrifice is love is easier to keep captive through threats against others.

The rewrite therefore needs both:

- operational reason the enemy chooses Sanji
- emotional reason Sanji responds the way he does
