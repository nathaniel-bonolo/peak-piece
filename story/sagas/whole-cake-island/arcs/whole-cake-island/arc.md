# Whole Cake Island

Status: Draft

## Premise

Peak Whole Cake Island is not built around Sanji being forced into a Germa political marriage.

The current direction ties the arc to:

- Fishman Island's Gem extraction
- Luffy challenging Linlin's authority
- the approaching Tea Party
- Charlotte soul harvesting
- Sanji's capture
- Brook's personal history with Linlin
- Pudding's memory work

## Current causal spine

1. Fishman Island is under coercive Charlotte influence because of Gem extraction.
2. Luffy helps disrupt that control and takes part in damaging or seizing extraction capacity.
3. Linlin loses access to Gems needed for the coming Tea Party and must buy energy at a much worse price.
4. Luffy's act is both an economic injury and a precedent problem.
5. Charlotte intelligence evaluates the Straw Hats.
6. Luffy is considered high future value and worth allowing to grow.
7. Sanji is considered valuable now, comparatively isolatable, and operationally important to the crew.
8. Sanji is captured for the coming harvest and as leverage.
9. The Straw Hats have two reasons to enter Totto Land:
   - recover Sanji
   - fulfill the Fishman alliance commitment
10. Pudding becomes involved because memory extraction is part of the Charlotte intelligence and harvest system, not because she exists only as Sanji's bride.

This chain is still under review.

## Sanji's absence

The crew should materially feel the loss of their cook.

Potential effects:

- poor ration planning
- spoilage
- Luffy consuming reserves too quickly
- reduced food safety
- emergency docking

This can naturally force an early stop at Cacao Island without requiring the canon stonefish scene.

## Pudding

Peak Pudding should not require a "sweet girl -> evil reveal -> secretly good" structure.

She can genuinely help the Straw Hats while also remaining loyal to Linlin and reporting useful information.

Nami does not need to lose intelligence for Pudding's deception to work.
