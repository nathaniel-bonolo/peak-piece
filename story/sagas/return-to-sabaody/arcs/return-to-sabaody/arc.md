# Return to Sabaody

## Function

Return to Sabaody is primarily a **reunion**.

Fishman Island will be the first major post-timeskip lesson about what the crew still does not understand.

Return to Sabaody instead needs to establish:

> **The two years mattered.**

The crew is stronger, more capable, and more prepared.

The world also had two years to prepare for **them**.

## Emotional hook

The opening feeling is less:

> holy shit, the crew is back

and more:

> **What happened to everyone over the last two years?**

The audience should be curious about the changes before the arc turns those changes into a combat showcase.

## Opening focal character: Chopper

Chopper is the opening emotional anchor rather than the sole POV of the arc.

He approaches Sabaody with uncomplicated excitement about seeing his friends again.

He encounters the Fake Straw Hats first.

They are redesigned to be visually closer to the crew they are impersonating. Two years of unknown training gives Chopper a plausible reason to think his friends may have changed physically.

He is fooled briefly, not indefinitely.

The comedy gives way to memory:

> **These people do not feel like my friends.**

Small recollections of the actual crew let Chopper recognize the difference and leave to find the real Straw Hats.

Once the reunion begins, focal weight widens across the crew so the arc does not become "Chopper's Sabaody."

## Fake Straw Hats

The imposters can stay because the gag is useful, but their existence needs causality.

The Straw Hats vanished publicly at the height of their notoriety.

For two years:

- nobody knows what they look like now
- their reputation keeps spreading
- weaker pirates want access to the name
- scammers can exploit uncertainty

The imposters therefore use the Straw Hat brand to recruit protection, status, and followers.

They do **not** need matching Devil Fruits.

Identity-level plausibility is enough.

Chopper is smart. He is not socially wise enough to demand that somebody demonstrate their Fruit as biometric proof before he believes they changed during two years apart.

## Sabaody becomes the new Loguetown

After the original Sabaody disaster, the Marines stop treating the archipelago like a place pirates simply pass through unnoticed.

Sabaody is the last major gate before the descent toward Fishman Island and the New World.

It becomes a monitored pirate chokepoint in the same narrative family as Loguetown.

**Smoker is put in charge of the Marine operation there.**

That assignment fits him:

- he understands pirate behavior
- he has personal history with Luffy
- he has watched Luffy repeatedly escape
- he believes letting Luffy reach his potential is dangerous
- Sabaody lets him intercept crews before they disappear into the New World

Tashigi serves with him.

Sabaody is not transformed into one giant Marine fortress.

It remains a civilian / pirate archipelago under much heavier Marine surveillance, patrol, intelligence, and rapid-response presence.

## Why the Marines expect the Straw Hats back

The crew vanished.

The **Sunny remained**.

That tells the Marines the story may not be over.

Instead of destroying the ship, Smoker preserves it as bait.

The Marines seize / impound it, inspect it, inventory it, and remove portable valuables / contraband while keeping the vessel intact because the owners may eventually return for it.

This replaces Kuma standing guard for two years.

Nami returning to discover that the Marines inventoried / confiscated her valuables is allowed to be a genuine comedy beat.

The 2D3Y message creates another clue even if Marine intelligence does not perfectly decode every meaning.

Then, almost exactly two years after the disappearance, Brook publicly schedules a major concert.

That is enough to sharpen the return window.

Smoker prepares the ambush.

## Marine response

The Marine presence can include:

- local forces
- rapid-response units
- Pacifistas
- Sentomaru
- Tashigi
- Smoker

Their appearance should feel like the result of the operation rather than canon characters coincidentally spawning where the reunion happens.

Marine intelligence can also keep the Fake Straw Hats under surveillance instead of instantly cleaning them up because the scam attracts wanted pirates and creates useful noise around the real crew's expected return.

That does not require Smoker to like the imposters.

## The showcase

The reunion and the ambush overlap.

Avoid a clean videogame sequence of:

> weak Marine → Pacifista → Tashigi → Smoker → boss clear

Instead, the operation starts collapsing as real and fake Straw Hats, recruited pirates, Marines, Pacifistas, and returning crewmates keep crossing paths.

Different Straw Hats get short opportunities to reveal what changed.

Pacifistas that once represented catastrophe are now manageable.

Smoker and Tashigi matter because they remember the old crew well enough to understand the scale of the difference.

## Luffy and Smoker

The important payoff is not necessarily a clean knockout.

Smoker used to be able to corner Luffy through a combination of Logia advantage, experience, and Luffy's lack of answers.

Now Luffy can actually fight him.

Smoker gets to experience the growth directly.

The Straw Hats' objective remains:

> **reunite, reclaim the Sunny, leave.**

Escaping a prepared Smoker operation while recovering their ship can be a better victory than pausing the reunion to finish a ceremonial boss battle.

## Bridge to Fishman Island

Return to Sabaody says:

> **The training worked.**

Fishman Island then gets to say:

> **Good. Strength still does not solve every problem.**

That two-arc progression is intentional.
