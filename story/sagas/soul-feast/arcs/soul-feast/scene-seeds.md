# Soul Feast: Scene Seeds

## Wano has no mirrors

Early Wano arrival beat.

A Straw Hat notices residents using water reflections to check their appearance.

The obvious question:

> Don't you have mirrors?

Official local answer:

> Orochi banned them. Says they invite vanity.

A child immediately says something like:

> Nah. He just doesn't wanna see his ugly face.

People laugh.

Move on.

Do not explain Brulee.

Do not cut to Kaido staring meaningfully at a mirror.

The first scene is allowed to be a stupid Orochi joke.

### Backstage reason

Kaido ordered the restriction years earlier.

He knows [[characters/brulee/overview|Brulee]] exists and does not want convenient reflective infrastructure spread across his home territory.

Brulee still needs usable / attuned mirrors.

Kaido is not interested in discovering which civilian mirror she managed to prepare after an invasion starts.

Household mirrors become rare.

Large clean reflective decorative surfaces are discouraged or restricted.

Some polished objects may be deliberately dulled when practical.

Water still reflects because Kaido is not banning physics.

### Optional later confirmation

A Queen / Orochi / Kaido flashback can include the policy only if the scene already has a reason to show defensive requirements.

For example, Queen is listing fortification rules:

> Port inspections. Den Den Mushi registry. No civilian mirrors.

Orochi:

> Mirrors?

Kaido:

> No mirrors.

Then the conversation continues.

Even this confirmation is optional.

A reader who works it out independently has already received the payoff.

## Linlin extracts the Hyenas

Linlin sees wounded / pressured Beast troops around her.

This normally means easy battlefield soul yield.

She reaches for all of them.

The visual language promises a huge harvest.

Then the result is embarrassingly small.

The Hyenas are still grinning.

Linlin's reaction should be fast.

Not a lecture.

The explanation belongs in [[systems/soul-system|Soul System]].
