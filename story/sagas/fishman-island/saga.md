# Fishman Island Saga

## Current focus

Fishman Island is the current major development focus.

The arc is being rebuilt around:

- Gems
- Fishman labor and slavery
- Whitebeard's political legacy
- Linlin's post-Whitebeard deal
- Hordy's history inside the mines
- Shirahoshi's rebellion
- Genauche's extraction administration
- Neptune's compromise
- the Tea Party production surge
