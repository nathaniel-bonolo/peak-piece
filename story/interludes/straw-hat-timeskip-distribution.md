# Straw Hat Timeskip Distribution

Peak Piece is not required to preserve canon's one-Straw-Hat-per-island distribution.

A destination should exist because it develops that character's actual specialization and problems.

## Luffy

[[story/interludes/timeskip-haki-training|Amazon Lily / Rusukaina]]

- Kuja Haki specialists
- Boa handles Conqueror's
- Rusukaina tests application

## Robin

Revolutionary Army base

Kuma sends her directly.

Focus:

- history
- intelligence
- archives
- state secrecy
- political systems
- underground information

## Usopp

Revolutionary field environment

Focus:

- long-range combat
- reconnaissance
- sabotage
- traps
- asymmetric warfare
- preparation
- intelligence

## Sanji

Food / cuisine culture.

Exact location still open.

Focus:

- cooking
- nutrition
- logistics
- preservation
- rare ingredients
- crew support

## Nami

Current canon core is not being thrown away.

She needs expansion.

Exact timeskip rewrite remains open.

## Franky

Current canon core is acceptable unless a stronger rewrite opportunity appears.

## Design rule

Do not ask:

> Which canon island still needs a Straw Hat?

Ask:

> What does this character need to become better at, and where would people who know that actually exist?
