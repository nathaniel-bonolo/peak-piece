# Story Overview

## Long character thread: Luffy

[[characters/luffy/progression|Luffy]] does not stay psychologically static.

His early belief that piracy means freedom gradually becomes a question about power, responsibility, Roger, Shanks, and what his own flag should mean.

Skypiea is a major snapping point because Enel shows that domination is larger than piracy.

## Timeskip

Peak Piece does not preserve canon destinations merely for symmetry.

See [[story/interludes/straw-hat-timeskip-distribution|Straw Hat Timeskip Distribution]].

Current locked directions:

- Luffy -> [[story/interludes/timeskip-haki-training|Amazon Lily Haki Training]]
- Robin -> [[characters/robin/timeskip|Revolutionary Army base]]
- Usopp -> [[characters/usopp/timeskip|Revolutionary field environment]]
- Sanji -> food / cuisine culture, exact destination open

Robin and Usopp can share an organization while developing completely different specialties.

## Current developed arc chain

### Fishman Island

[[story/sagas/fishman-island/arcs/fishman-island/arc|Fishman Island]] establishes:

- the Gem economy
- Fishman extraction and slavery
- Whitebeard's political legacy
- Linlin's strategic interest in Fishman Island
- Hordy, Shirahoshi, Genauche, Neptune, and Jinbe's competing approaches to liberation
- the disruption of Linlin's Gem supply

It also deepens Luffy's question about power by contrasting what Whitebeard and Linlin each do with overwhelming pirate authority.

### Whole Cake Island

[[story/sagas/whole-cake-island/arcs/whole-cake-island/arc|Whole Cake Island]] follows from Linlin's Gem loss, Fishman resistance, Sanji's capture, the Tea Party, and the Charlotte soul economy.

### Soul Feast

[[story/sagas/soul-feast/arcs/soul-feast/arc|Soul Feast]] turns Linlin's resource pressure and Kaido's prepared military state into a Yonko-vs-Yonko war engineered by the Straw Hats and Law.

## Earlier story

East Blue, Skypiea, and many pre-timeskip arcs have not yet been fully rewritten at chapter level.

## Timeskip character development

Robin and Usopp both enter the Revolutionary Army through different pipelines. Robin goes directly to base and develops through choice, scholarship, and intelligence. Usopp spends weeks or months in Betty's siege learning asymmetric warfare before returning to base and unexpectedly meeting Robin.
